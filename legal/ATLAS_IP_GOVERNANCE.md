# ATLAS IP GOVERNANCE STANDARDS

## Version

1.0

## Purpose

This document defines how Atlas intellectual property is governed, approved, licensed, modified, and protected.

It does **not** replace the Atlas Character Specification, Brand Bible, or Animation Bible. Those documents remain the creative source of truth. This document controls how changes and usage approvals are managed.

---

## Ownership

Atlas is the official mascot, AI assistant, and brand ambassador of Collectiverse Studio.

Atlas, the Atlas character system, character design, production rules, canonical prompts, visual identity, expressions, poses, animation behavior, brand voice, and related materials are proprietary intellectual property of Collectiverse Studio / Gregory Bell unless otherwise stated in a signed written agreement.

---

## Source of Truth Rule

The repository `CollectiverseStudio/atlas-ip` is the official source of truth for Atlas IP.

No external AI chat, generated image, video tool output, contractor file, marketing asset, merchandise prototype, social post, or app implementation overrides this repository.

If another source conflicts with this repository, this repository controls.

---

## Change Control

Atlas may not be redesigned casually.

Any proposed change to Atlas must identify:

- What is changing
- Why the change is necessary
- Which canonical document is affected
- Whether the change affects existing images, comics, videos, merchandise, or app assets
- Whether the change creates trademark, copyright, trade dress, or brand confusion risk
- Whether the version number must be incremented

No change is approved unless it is committed to this repository.

---

The following elements are protected and should not be changed without formal approval:

- Name: Atlas

- Metallic blue accent system
- Single centered gold five-point star
- Black visor
- Green crescent eyes
- Small orange mouth

- Dark badge / Collectiverse chest emblem
- Friendly explorer personality
- Collector-first voice
- Non-aggressive behavior rules
- Respectful collectible handling rules

---

## Non-Derivative Character Rule

Atlas must remain unique to Collectiverse Studio.

Atlas must not be intentionally designed, prompted, posed, animated, merchandised, or marketed to resemble a third-party character, mascot, robot, superhero, cartoon, game character, film character, toy line, or franchise.

References to broad production quality may be used internally as shorthand, but final prompts and vendor briefs should favor original language describing Atlas's own canonical traits.

---

## Public vs. Confidential Materials

This repository is public unless changed by Collectiverse Studio.

Do not commit sensitive information, including:

- Private legal strategy
- Attorney-client communications
- Registration account credentials
- Private contract terms
- Vendor banking or payment data
- Unreleased partner agreements
- Raw identity documents
- Trade secrets not intended for public release

Public-facing legal notices, usage standards, and template language may be stored here.

---

## Contributor Rules

All contributors must:

- Preserve Atlas canon
- Avoid derivative character generation
- Avoid unapproved redesigns
- Use approved character prompts and references
- Keep Collectiverse branding intact
- Avoid political, offensive, violent, adult, hateful, or misleading uses
- Submit proposed changes through documented files or pull requests when possible

---

## Approval Levels

### Routine Content

Examples: social captions, educational tips, simple pose prompts, short comic ideas.

Allowed if consistent with the Brand Bible and Character Spec.

### Controlled Content

Examples: new outfits, recurring props, app assistant behavior, video templates, merchandise mockups.

Requires review against Character Spec, Brand Bible, Animation Bible, and AI governance standards.

### Restricted Content

Examples: redesigns, new character variants, license grants, legal claims, trademark usage, toy manufacturing specs, commercial partner approvals.

Requires explicit Collectiverse Studio approval before use.

---

## Enforcement Position

Collectiverse Studio should maintain evidence of Atlas creation, revisions, published use, and enforcement actions.

Recommended evidence archive:

- Dated commits
- Final artwork exports
- Source files
- Prompt logs where useful
- Published URLs
- Marketing screenshots
- Vendor approvals
- Takedown notices
- Trademark/copyright filings

---

## Practical Rule

When uncertain, do not change Atlas. Add guidance, propose options, or create a draft for review.
