### Typography Rules
- Header: Always ALL CAPS "ATLAS TIP #NNN"
- Bullets: Start with green checkmark ✓ or bullet •
- 3-5 bullet points per post (never fewer than 3, never more than 5)
- Each bullet: max 15 words
- Bold key terms within bullets
- No paragraph text — always bulleted/listed

---

## ✅ Required Elements

1. **Header badge** — "ATLAS TIP #[NUMBER]" in navy/blue, top of image
2. **Atlas character** — Holding or interacting with relevant collectible/tool
3. **3-5 tip bullets** — Clear, actionable advice
4. **Footer** — Collectiverse logo (left) + @collectiverse handle (right)
5. **Clean background** — White or light pastel (never dark for tips)
6. **Consistent numbering** — Sequential tip numbers across series
7. **One clear topic** — Each tip post covers ONE subject

---

## 🔲 Optional Elements

- Tip title/subtitle between header and bullets
- Small icons next to each bullet point
- Accent divider line (gold) between sections
- "Save this post! 🔖" CTA text
- Category badge (e.g., "GRADING" "STORAGE" "BUYING")
- QR code linking to full article/video
- "Part 1 of 3" series indicator
- Small collectible illustrations next to bullets

---

## ✔️ Do's

- ✅ Make each tip actionable — reader should be able to DO something
- ✅ Keep Atlas's pose relevant to the topic (holding cards for card tips, etc.)
- ✅ Use bold for the most important word in each bullet
- ✅ Number tips sequentially — creates collectibility of the content itself
- ✅ Use green checkmarks for "do this" tips, red X for "avoid this" tips
- ✅ Make the tip title a question when possible ("How do I...?")
- ✅ Ensure tips are beginner-friendly — this is educational content
- ✅ End with a "bonus" or "pro tip" as the last bullet for advanced collectors

---

## ❌ Don'ts

- ❌ Don't use more than 5 bullet points (information overload)
- ❌ Don't write paragraphs — always bullets/lists
- ❌ Don't use dark backgrounds (this is educational = light & clean)
- ❌ Don't make Atlas larger than the text area (text is primary content)
- ❌ Never skip the tip number — consistency builds series recognition
- ❌ Don't use jargon without explanation in beginner tips
- ❌ Don't place text over the character zone
- ❌ Avoid humor that undermines the educational value
