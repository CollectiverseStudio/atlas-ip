# Atlas Version 1.0 Canonical Style Guide

## Purpose

This guide defines the approved Atlas v1.0 visual system for artists, AI agents, developers, merchandisers, and production partners.

Atlas should remain visually consistent across Collectiverse web apps, mobile apps, social campaigns, comics, animation, merchandise, licensing materials, and investor or partner presentations.

---

## Identity

| Field | Standard |
|---|---|
| Name | Atlas |
| Brand Role | Collectiverse mascot, guide, explorer, educator, ambassador |
| Personality | Curious, helpful, smart, friendly, adventurous, trustworthy |
| Mission | Help collectors protect, authenticate, organize, understand, and grow their collections |
| Brand Line | Every item has a story |
| Version | Atlas v1.0 |

---

## Character Design

Atlas is a premium friendly robot, not a generic android. His design balances collectible-toy appeal, technology credibility, and warmth.

Required features:

- Large round white head.
- Black visor face.
- Neon emerald crescent eyes.
- Small friendly mouth.
- Metallic gold star antenna.
- White armor shell.
- Metallic Collectiverse-blue accents.
- Piano-black joints and hands.
- Silver circular ear modules.
- Compact torso.
- Oversized boots.
- Official blue hexagonal white `C` chest emblem.

---

## Official Chest Emblem

The current chest emblem is final.

Use: `logos/atlas-chest-emblem-v1.svg`

Rules:

- Do not redesign.
- Do not replace with shield, vault, connected-node, compass, or core variants.
- Do not add extra icons.
- Do not rotate.
- Do not stretch.
- Do not recolor outside approved material lighting.
- Keep centered on torso.

The emblem should read as a clean blue hexagonal badge with a white `C`, not as a detailed infographic symbol.

## Proportion Rules

Approximate reference:

- Total height: 100%.
- Head: 45%.
- Body and legs: 55%.
- Chest emblem: 12% of total standing height.
- Hands: oversized enough to read clearly in small UI assets.
- Boots: oversized and rounded for friendly stability.

Atlas should never become tall, thin, militarized, or human-like.

## Scale Rules

Atlas must remain recognizable at small sizes.

Minimum recognition anchors at small scale:

- White round head.
- Black visor.
- Green crescent eyes.
- Gold star.
- Blue body accents.
- Chest emblem where size permits.

For favicon or tiny icon use, prioritize head, eyes, and star over full-body detail.

---

## Do

- Use official emblem only.
- Preserve proportions.
- Preserve color palette.
- Preserve star antenna.
- Preserve face and eye design.
- Use premium rendering quality.
- Use Atlas as a guide, educator, and ambassador.
- Make Atlas feel helpful, intelligent, and trustworthy.

---

## Do Not

- Change the emblem.
- Change eye color.
- Change or remove star antenna.
- Add weapons.
- Make Atlas angry, scary, or villainous.
- Stretch or flatten the head.
- Alter body proportions.
- Add extra features without approval.
- Use rough, low-quality, or inconsistent renders.
