## Purpose

This file defines the immutable visual traits for the Atlas v2.0 master image.

## Mandatory Traits

- Premium Pixar-quality 3D CGI mascot.
- Chibi robot proportions.
- Large glossy white rounded helmet.
- Metallic blue helmet side accents.
- Centered metallic gold five-point star on top of helmet.
- Black continuous rounded visor.
- Green glowing crescent eyes only.
- No eyebrows.
- No nose.
- Small orange rounded-rectangle mouth.
- Silver circular ear discs.
- Compact white robot torso.
- Metallic blue shoulder, hip, knee, and trim accents.
- Dark graphite hexagonal chest badge with blue `C`.
- Black segmented five-finger hands.
- White rounded boots with metallic blue trim and black soles.
- Friendly neutral expression.
- Full body visible.
- Transparent background preferred for production.

## Rejection Criteria

Reject generated assets if any of these appear:

- Cape.
- Green suit replacing white/blue body.
- Human face.
- Human eyes.
- Circular eyes with pupils.
- Eyebrows.
- Missing chest badge.
- Missing gold star.
- Incorrect star shape.
- Wrong boot style.
- Angry, violent, political, threatening, or cruel expression.

## Source-of-Truth Rule

After approval, all A002+ assets must be generated from or checked against `canonical/atlas-master-v2.0.png`.

No downstream asset may redefine Atlas.
