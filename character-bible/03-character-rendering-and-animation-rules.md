# Collectiverse Character Rendering and Animation Rules

## Purpose

This document defines how Collectiverse characters should be rendered, animated, posed, simplified, merchandised, and generated by artists or AI systems.

## Global Rules

1. Atlas is the primary mascot and must remain the visual anchor of Collectiverse.
2. Every character must remain original and must not imitate an existing franchise character, superhero, mascot, robot, cartoon explorer, or villain design.
3. Characters must remain family-friendly while still supporting serious collector themes.
4. Physical collectibles must always be handled carefully.
5. Villains may be visually interesting, but fraudulent or harmful behavior must never be endorsed.
6. Visual clarity matters more than excessive detail.
7. Silhouettes must remain readable at small sizes.
8. Each character must retain their role-specific tools, materials, posture, and emotional pattern.

## Hero Rendering Rules

Hero characters should use open posture, readable eyes, visible hands, friendly facial shapes, rounded forms, and approachable materials. Even the more serious heroes, such as Sterling and Forge, should feel trustworthy rather than intimidating.

Atlas should be the most expressive character. Pixel should be the fastest and smallest. Ink should feel soft and story-driven. Sterling should feel refined and precise. Forge should feel broad and protective. Porter should feel practical and mobile. Echo should feel quiet, graceful, and memory-focused.

## Villain Rendering Rules

Villains should represent threats to collecting trust. Use sharper shapes, hidden hands, closed posture, asymmetry, suspicious props, shadowed faces, and more controlled or distorted materials.

The Director should feel controlled and theatrical. The Counterfeiter should feel slippery and deceptive. The Archivist should feel secretive and dusty. The Broker should feel polished and predatory. The Smuggler should feel concealed and evasive. The Restorer should feel beautiful but deceptive. The Hacker should feel unstable and electric. The Hoarder should feel overloaded and trapped by objects.

## Expression Rules

Every major character should support the following expressions:

- Neutral
- Happy
- Concerned
- Suspicious
- Thinking
- Shocked
- Determined
- Angry
- Embarrassed
- Confused
- Proud
- Tired

Atlas must have the widest expression range. Villains may have narrower ranges, but they still need readable eyes, brow shapes, and mouth behavior.

## Movement Rules

Atlas moves with energetic curiosity.
Pixel moves with quick digital hops and zips.
Ink moves smoothly and thoughtfully.
Sterling moves with controlled precision.
Forge moves heavily but carefully.
Porter moves efficiently and practically.
Echo moves softly and attentively.

The Director moves slowly and deliberately.
The Counterfeiter moves evasively.
The Archivist moves with ritual precision.
The Broker moves smoothly and invasively.
The Smuggler moves quietly and diagonally.
The Restorer moves delicately and surgically.
The Hacker moves twitchily and unpredictably.
The Hoarder moves slowly and defensively.

## AI Image Generation Rules

When generating Collectiverse character images, prompts should include:

- Character name
- Role in Collectiverse
- Physical silhouette
- Face and eye behavior
- Clothing and material language
- Color palette direction
- Pose or action
- Emotional state
- Explicit note that the character is original Collectiverse IP
- Explicit instruction not to copy or reference existing characters or franchises

Do not prompt in the style of named studios, named artists, specific franchises, or existing mascots.

## Merchandise Rules

Merchandise versions may simplify details but must preserve:

- Character silhouette
- Face shape and eye language
- Main color identity
- Core accessory or emblem
- Personality posture
- Role-specific visual cues

Small-format items such as stickers or icons may remove minor surface texture, but should not remove the character's identity.

## Legal and IP Protection Rules

Every official character image, model sheet, animation, comic strip, sticker, toy, or product asset should be traceable back to this character bible.

Do not create uncontrolled alternate versions. If alternate costumes, seasonal variants, event skins, or special editions are created, they must be labeled as variants and must preserve the canonical core design.

## Canon Rule

The character profiles in this folder are the baseline. Future art may refine details, but it should not contradict the characters' physical appearance, role, personality, strengths, weaknesses, or visual identity.
