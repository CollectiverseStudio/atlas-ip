# Character Rendering and Animation Rules

Status: CANON

Purpose: Use this file for AI image generation, comics, animation, merchandise, and QA approval.

---

# Universal Character Rules

1. Every character must be recognizable by silhouette.
2. Every character must have a unique physical shape.
3. Every character must represent a value, not just a job.
4. No Guardian may look like a generic robot.
5. No adversary may look like a generic comic-book villain.
6. Atlas remains the emotional heart of the universe.
7. The collector remains the true hero of the story.
8. Do not prompt in the style of named studios, named artists, existing franchises, or existing mascots.

---

# Guardian Shared Visual Language

Guardians share these traits:

- Premium stylized cinematic CGI design.
- Rounded, safe, welcoming shapes.
- White ceramic base materials.
- Guardian Blue technology glow.
- No weapons.
- No military styling.
- No tactical armor.
- No horror lighting.
- No intimidation.
- Body language that invites trust.

Each Guardian also has a distinct accent color and silhouette:

- Atlas: rounded explorer silhouette, Guardian Blue, gold star, Atlas Lens.
- Pixel: spherical floating silhouette, Electric Cyan.
- Ink: tall oval expressive silhouette, Deep Indigo.
- Sterling: compact hexagonal precision silhouette, Brushed Silver.
- Forge: triangular builder silhouette, Copper Bronze.
- Porter: broad square protective silhouette, Forest Green.
- Echo: tall rectangular scholar silhouette, Royal Violet.

---

# Adversary Shared Visual Language

Adversaries should feel believable, sophisticated, and philosophically opposed to the Guardians.

Avoid:

- horns
- skulls
- glowing red eyes
- monster designs
- combat armor
- evil capes
- exaggerated villain costumes
- generic dark lord design

Use:

- restraint
- elegance
- controlled environments
- symbolic props
- archive rooms
- private galleries
- tailored clothing
- professional tools
- psychological tension

---

# Image Generation Requirements

Every character image must include:

- Full body unless marked as portrait.
- Clear readable silhouette.
- No text embedded in the image unless specifically requested.
- Clean lighting.
- No unauthorized logos.
- No weapons.
- No visual references that resemble known copyrighted characters.
- No superhero cape for Atlas.
- No astronaut suit for Atlas.

# QA Rejection Rules

Reject any character art if:

- Atlas looks like an astronaut.
- Atlas looks like a superhero.
- Atlas has a cape instead of the explorer mantle.
- Atlas lacks the star, Lens, mantle, blue eyes, or warm expression.
- Guardians look like recolored versions of Atlas.
- Adversaries look cartoonishly evil.
- Any character carries weapons.
- Any image looks derivative of existing franchises.
- The artwork makes collectibles more important than people.

---

# Approval Threshold

A production image must score 95/100 or higher to become canon.

Scoring:

- Identity: 25
- Silhouette: 20
- Personality: 15
- Canon accuracy: 15
- Animation usability: 10
- Merchandise usability: 10
- Emotional appeal: 5

No exceptions.
