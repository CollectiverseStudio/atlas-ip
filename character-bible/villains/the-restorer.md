# The Restorer — The Beautiful Lie

## Core Identity

The Restorer represents undisclosed alteration, cosmetic deception, hidden repair, artificial improvement, condition manipulation, and the danger of making an item look better while hiding what happened to it.

The Restorer is not against preservation. The Restorer is against disclosure.

## Overall Physical Appearance

The Restorer is elegant, clean, and unsettlingly perfect. The silhouette is slender and graceful, with long arms, precise hands, and an immaculate outfit. At first glance the character looks refined and artistic. On closer inspection, the perfection feels artificial.

The Restorer should visually contrast with Forge. Forge protects condition honestly. The Restorer manipulates appearance secretly.

## Head and Face

The Restorer has a smooth symmetrical face that is almost too flawless. The eyes are calm and clinical, with a faint reflective shine. The eyebrows are thin, precise, and carefully shaped.

The mouth forms a soft, controlled smile. The smile should feel reassuring until the viewer notices it never changes naturally.

## Body Structure

The Restorer is slim and controlled. The arms are long and graceful. The hands are extremely important: delicate, gloved, precise, and almost surgical in movement.

The character stands upright and close to objects, leaning in with careful attention.

## Clothing and Gear

The Restorer wears pristine restoration-studio clothing.

Canonical elements may include:

- Smooth apron, coat, or fitted work garment.
- Fine gloves.
- Soft brushes.
- Tiny bottles with abstract labels.
- Polishing cloth.
- Repair tools shown only symbolically.
- Hidden disclosure tag kept out of sight.

Avoid showing real techniques for deceptive alteration.

## Color and Surface

The palette should include ivory, pale gray, pearl, surgical teal, muted lavender, and subtle silver. Surfaces are too clean, too smooth, and almost sterile.

The character may have faint glossy highlights that make them feel artificial.

## Movement and Animation

The Restorer moves slowly and precisely. Every hand motion is beautiful and unnervingly controlled. They smooth surfaces, align edges, and polish away evidence.

When confronted about disclosure, the movements stop. The Restorer becomes icy and still.

## Personality

The Restorer is refined, perfectionist, secretive, and proud. They believe appearance matters more than honesty. They justify deception by claiming they make objects “better.”

The Restorer dislikes damage, flaws, wear, and messy truth.

## Strengths

- Fine detail work.
- Calm precision.
- Cosmetic manipulation.
- Persuasive language about beauty.
- Hiding visible flaws.

## Weaknesses

- Cannot tolerate honest imperfection.
- Becomes defensive when asked for disclosure.
- Underestimates collectors who value originality.
- Leaves subtle evidence detectable by careful inspection.

## Relationship to Forge and Sterling

Forge preserves honestly. Sterling evaluates honestly. The Restorer hides alteration to influence condition and value.

## Signature Poses

- Holding a soft brush near a collectible.
- Polishing a surface while hiding a disclosure note.
- Looking serene while Atlas looks suspicious.
- Freezing when Sterling asks, “Was this disclosed?”
- Holding up a flawless-looking item with too much pride.

## Voice and Speech

The Restorer speaks softly, elegantly, and defensively.

Typical tone:

- “I only improved what time damaged.”
- “Collectors prefer beauty.”
- “Disclosure ruins the illusion.”

## Never Allowed

- Do not provide technical restoration fraud instructions.
- Do not portray honest restoration as evil.
- Do not make The Restorer dirty or chaotic.
- Do not let cosmetic perfection replace truth in Collectiverse values.
