# The Broker Villain Profile

## Role

The Broker represents predatory deals, marketplace manipulation, pressure selling, unfair offers, hidden fees, and collectors being exploited when they do not know the true value of what they own.

## Physical Appearance

The Broker is polished, compact, and predatory. The body is not large, but the posture is forward-leaning and invasive. The silhouette should feel like a sharp suit wrapped around a trap. The shoulders are narrow, the hands are quick, and the head angles slightly downward when speaking to others.

The face is smooth and controlled. The eyes are narrow, calculating, and always looking for weakness. The smile is professional but empty. The teeth may be too even. The chin is pointed enough to create a transactional edge.

The Broker wears refined clothing with subtle marketplace and auction cues: bid paddles, price tags, fine lapels, hidden contract pockets, tiny calculator-like details, or a watch that is checked too often. The palette should be dark, expensive, and slightly cold, with a sharp accent color that suggests urgency or warning.

## Personality and Behavior

The Broker is charming, impatient, persuasive, and predatory. The Broker makes every conversation feel like a deal that must happen now. This villain compliments collectors just enough to lower their guard, then applies pressure.

The Broker moves smoothly but quickly. Hands are always adjusting papers, sliding offers forward, tapping numbers, or concealing terms. The Broker stands too close and speaks as if doing the collector a favor.

## Strengths

The Broker excels at negotiation traps, false urgency, underpricing collections, exploiting grief or confusion, hiding true value, and manipulating marketplaces.

## Weaknesses

The Broker is vulnerable to transparency, comps, fair valuation, buyer-seller protections, Sterling's pricing discipline, and Atlas's ability to slow the collector down before a bad deal is accepted.

## Never Do

The Broker should never be shown as a legitimate business role. Collectiverse can have honest sellers and dealers; The Broker represents predatory exploitation only.
