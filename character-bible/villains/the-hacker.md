# The Hacker — The Record Corruptor

## Core Identity

The Hacker represents corrupted records, manipulated ownership data, fake digital claims, broken metadata, account abuse, unauthorized changes, and attacks against the digital trust layer of Collectiverse.

The Hacker does not need to touch a collectible to damage its value. They attack the record that proves what it is.

## Overall Physical Appearance

The Hacker is thin, twitchy, electric, and visually unstable. The silhouette is narrow with sharp elbows, hunched shoulders, quick hands, and screen-glow accents. The character should feel like a glitch in the Collectiverse system.

The design should not be a generic hooded hacker stereotype. It must feel specific to collectible records, ownership claims, metadata corruption, and provenance disruption.

## Head and Face

The Hacker has an angular head with uneven digital glow across the face. The eyes are bright, restless, and slightly mismatched, as if one is reading code while the other watches the room.

The eyebrows are jagged and expressive. The mouth may appear as a thin grin, broken line, or glitch-like smirk. The face should feel intelligent but unstable.

## Body Structure

The body is slim and tense. The arms are long and quick. Fingers are narrow and constantly moving, tapping invisible screens, pulling digital threads, or scrambling records.

The legs are thin and restless. The Hacker rarely stands still without some small twitch or flicker.

## Clothing and Gear

The Hacker wears tech-corruption attire.

Canonical elements may include:

- Asymmetrical jacket.
- Glowing broken-line patterns.
- Corrupted tag or broken QR motif.
- Small floating screen fragments.
- Fingerless digital gloves.
- Abstract code-like scarf or cable-like trim.

Avoid real hacking tool logos, real exploit references, or instructional details.

## Color and Surface

The palette should include black, electric green, acid blue, violet, gray, and corrupted red accents. Glows can flicker but should not overpower the character’s readable form.

Surfaces may look pixelated, broken, or digitally torn.

## Movement and Animation

The Hacker moves in abrupt, jittery bursts. They may freeze, flicker, then suddenly appear in a different pose. Hands move faster than the body. Screens and record fragments may stutter around them.

When blocked by secure systems, The Hacker becomes irritated and erratic.

## Personality

The Hacker is brilliant, restless, arrogant, and disruptive. They believe digital records are only as real as whoever controls the system.

The Hacker enjoys creating doubt. Their goal is not always theft; sometimes it is chaos, confusion, and loss of confidence.

## Strengths

- Metadata manipulation.
- Digital misdirection.
- Fast pattern disruption.
- Exploiting weak security.
- Creating false ownership confusion.
- Psychological disruption through uncertainty.

## Weaknesses

- Impatient.
- Overconfident.
- Leaves traces when moving too fast.
- Struggles against layered verification, audit logs, and human review.
- Underestimates Atlas and Pixel working together.

## Relationship to Pixel and Echo

Pixel detects data inconsistencies quickly. Echo senses record continuity and provenance gaps. Together, they are The Hacker’s worst problem.

## Signature Poses

- Tapping on invisible floating screens.
- Laughing as record fragments scatter.
- Looking shocked when an audit trail exposes them.
- Holding a broken QR-like symbol.
- Flickering at the edge of a digital vault.

## Voice and Speech

The Hacker speaks quickly, mockingly, and with glitch-like rhythm.

Typical tone:

- “Records are just stories with passwords.”
- “Change the data, change the truth.”
- “Trust me — or don’t. That’s the fun.”

## Never Allowed

- Do not provide real hacking instructions.
- Do not include real exploit names as guidance.
- Do not make The Hacker a generic hoodie stereotype.
- Do not imply Collectiverse security is weak by default.
