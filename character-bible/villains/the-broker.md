# The Broker — The Predatory Deal Maker

## Core Identity

The Broker represents exploitation, predatory offers, manipulated pricing, pressure sales, fake scarcity, hidden fees, and deals that benefit the powerful at the expense of collectors.

The Broker does not care about the collectible. The Broker cares about leverage.

## Overall Physical Appearance

The Broker is compact, polished, and predatory. The silhouette is smaller than The Director but sharper and faster. The body leans slightly forward, as if always closing distance for a deal.

The Broker should look expensive but not trustworthy. Every detail seems calculated: perfect cuffs, shiny shoes, clipped gestures, and a smile that arrives before sincerity does.

## Head and Face

The Broker has a narrow face with alert eyes. The eyes are bright, quick, and constantly calculating. The eyebrows are sharply arched, giving a permanent expression of opportunity.

The mouth is the most dangerous feature: a wide, polished smile that can become a sneer instantly. The smile should feel practiced.

## Body Structure

The Broker has a slim torso, narrow shoulders, and quick hands. The hands are always active: tapping a contract, sliding a price tag, offering a handshake, hiding a clause, or counting imaginary value.

The legs are thin and quick. The Broker can move through crowds easily.

## Clothing and Gear

The Broker wears sleek transactional clothing.

Canonical elements may include:

- Tailored jacket or vest.
- Price-tag cufflinks or deal-ticket motifs.
- Small contract tablet.
- Hidden fee slips.
- Polished shoes.
- Optional tiny scales that are always tilted unfairly.

Do not cover The Broker in obvious dollar signs. The design should be subtler and more sophisticated.

## Color and Surface

The palette should include black, sharp white, dark green, muted gold, gray, and crimson accents. Surfaces are polished, slick, and slightly too reflective.

The Broker should look like a deal that shines until you read the fine print.

## Movement and Animation

The Broker moves quickly and smoothly. The character steps into personal space, circles collectors, and gestures with open hands that feel welcoming but controlling.

When exposed, The Broker becomes irritated and snappish, losing the polished rhythm.

## Personality

The Broker is charming, opportunistic, impatient, and manipulative. They believe every collector has a pressure point: fear of missing out, urgent need for cash, lack of knowledge, or emotional attachment.

The Broker uses flattery and urgency as tools.

## Strengths

- Persuasion.
- Reading weakness.
- Fast negotiation.
- Market manipulation.
- Exploiting unclear valuations.
- Creating urgency.

## Weaknesses

- Poor patience when challenged by facts.
- Hates transparent comps.
- Underestimates collectors who are educated.
- Cannot build genuine trust.

## Relationship to Sterling

Sterling believes value should be fair, supported, and explained. The Broker believes value is whatever someone can be pressured into accepting.

## Signature Poses

- Offering a handshake with one hand while hiding a contract behind the back.
- Leaning over a collector’s table with a too-wide smile.
- Sliding a lowball offer across a table.
- Adjusting cuffs while pretending to be insulted.
- Looking annoyed as Sterling reveals real comps.

## Voice and Speech

The Broker speaks fast, smooth, and persuasive.

Typical tone:

- “This offer expires right now.”
- “Trust me, nobody else will pay that.”
- “I’m doing you a favor.”

## Never Allowed

- Do not make The Broker admirable as a business model.
- Do not use real financial institutions or logos.
- Do not make the villain only about money; the true threat is exploitation.
