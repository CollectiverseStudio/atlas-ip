# The Hacker Villain Profile

## Role

The Hacker represents digital intrusion, corrupted records, manipulated ownership data, stolen credentials, broken trust signals, and attacks against the systems that protect Collectiverse records.

## Physical Appearance

The Hacker is thin, twitchy, electric, and visually unstable. The silhouette should feel broken into sharp angles: narrow shoulders, bent elbows, long fingers, raised collar, dangling cables, cracked-screen motifs, or glitch-like clothing edges.

The face is restless and partially lit by screen glow. The eyes are bright, uneven, and intense, as if reflecting multiple monitors. The mouth may form quick smirks, nervous grins, or muttered concentration. Hair, hood, visor, or digital fragments may obscure parts of the face.

The outfit should include cracked display textures, glitch marks, loose straps, corrupted icon patterns, keyboard-like tabs, and small device fragments. The palette should be dark with electric accents. The design must remain readable and not become a pile of generic cyberpunk details.

## Personality and Behavior

The Hacker is restless, arrogant, impatient, and addicted to disruption. This villain enjoys bypassing trust more than owning objects. The Hacker sees systems as puzzles to break and people as weak points.

Movement should include quick jerks, sudden stillness, finger tapping, screen swiping, and glitchy posture shifts. The Hacker rarely stands calmly.

## Strengths

The Hacker excels at credential theft, record manipulation, fake ownership signals, data corruption, marketplace account abuse, and exploiting weak security.

## Weaknesses

The Hacker is exposed by strong authentication, audit logs, immutable records, anomaly detection, Pixel's digital awareness, Echo's record integrity, and Atlas's insistence on verification.

## Never Do

The Hacker should never be portrayed as a harmless prankster. The character represents attacks on trust, ownership, and collector safety.
