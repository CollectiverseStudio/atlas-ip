# Collectiverse Villains

This folder contains the canonical villain-side character profiles for the Collectiverse universe.

The villains represent threats to collectors, marketplaces, provenance, ownership, condition, value, and trust.

## Characters

- `the-director.md` — manipulation, control, false narratives
- `the-counterfeiter.md` — fake authenticity and forged identity
- `the-archivist.md` — hoarded knowledge and locked-away history
- `the-broker.md` — predatory valuation and exploitative deals
- `the-smuggler.md` — hidden movement and broken chain of custody
- `the-restorer.md` — undisclosed alteration and cosmetic deception
- `the-hacker.md` — corrupted records and digital manipulation
- `the-hoarder.md` — possession without stewardship

## Villain Rule

Villains should be compelling and memorable, but Collectiverse remains family-friendly. They should feel dangerous to trust, provenance, and collector confidence — not graphically violent.
