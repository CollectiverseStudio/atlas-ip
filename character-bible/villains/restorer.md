# The Restorer Villain Profile

## Role

The Restorer represents undisclosed restoration, condition deception, artificial improvement, cleaning fraud, trimming, recoloring, repaired damage hidden from buyers, and any attempt to make altered collectibles appear untouched.

## Physical Appearance

The Restorer is elegant but unsettling. The body is slim, precise, and almost surgical. The posture is upright and delicate, with hands held carefully in front of the body. Everything about the character appears clean, controlled, and too perfect.

The face is smooth and composed, with calm eyes that rarely blink. The eyebrows are fine and controlled. The mouth is small and faintly pleased, as if admiring a flaw no one else can see. The character should be attractive in a cold way, but never warm.

The outfit may include white or pale gloves, a long fitted apron or coat, fine tools, brushes, adhesive bottles, color swatches, magnification lenses, and immaculate sleeves. Surfaces should be polished, spotless, and suspiciously pristine. The palette should include pale whites, clinical grays, soft gold, and one unsettling accent.

## Personality and Behavior

The Restorer is perfectionistic, patient, vain, and deceptive. This villain does not see alteration as fraud when the result looks beautiful. The Restorer believes appearance matters more than truth.

Movement should be slow, delicate, and precise. The Restorer touches objects with ritual control, examines edges, adjusts light, and smiles faintly when a flaw disappears.

## Strengths

The Restorer excels at hiding defects, altering surfaces, recoloring, trimming, pressing, cleaning, and making damaged items look deceptively original.

## Weaknesses

The Restorer is exposed by disclosure requirements, UV inspection, condition history, before-and-after records, Forge's preservation ethics, Sterling's appraisal discipline, and Atlas's suspicion of anything too perfect.

## Never Do

The Restorer should not imply that legitimate disclosed conservation is evil. The villain represents undisclosed deceptive alteration.
