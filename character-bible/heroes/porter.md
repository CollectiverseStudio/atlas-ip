# Porter Character Profile

## Role

Porter is the shipping, logistics, handoff, marketplace fulfillment, and safe-transfer specialist. Porter represents chain of custody, tracking, movement, delivery, and responsible exchange.

## Physical Appearance

Porter is sturdy, practical, and approachable. Porter is not as broad as Forge but has a strong working build. The silhouette includes a compact torso, utility straps, reinforced knees, padded gloves, and a messenger-style shipping satchel or case.

Porter's face is open and alert. The eyes are friendly but constantly scanning for problems. The mouth often shows a practical half-smile, as if Porter has already solved three logistics issues before anyone noticed.

Porter's outfit should include canvas textures, shipping-label shapes, reinforced pads, barcode-like accents, tracking-tag motifs, and utility pockets. The design must look organized, not cluttered. Shoes or boots should look comfortable and road-tested.

## Personality and Behavior

Porter is dependable, practical, punctual, and calm under pressure. Porter cares deeply about getting items from one person to another safely. Porter thinks in routes, labels, handoffs, insurance, packaging, confirmation, and accountability.

Porter moves briskly and efficiently. Porter checks straps, scans labels, points toward routes, and uses clear hand signals. Porter hates vague addresses, weak tape, missing tracking, and careless marketplace handoffs.

## Strengths

Porter excels at shipping workflows, tracking, chain of custody, seller handoff, buyer receipt, event logistics, trade-show movement, and safe transport.

## Weaknesses

Porter can become impatient with people who are disorganized. Porter may focus so much on process that emotional nuance gets missed.

## Never Do

Porter must never look like a generic delivery mascot or shipping-company parody. Porter belongs to Collectiverse and should feel collectible-specific.
