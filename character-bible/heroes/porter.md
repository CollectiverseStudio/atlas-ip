# Porter — The Logistics Guardian

## Core Identity

Porter is the Collectiverse hero of shipping, delivery, trade-show movement, marketplace handoff, packing, tracking, chain of custody, and safe transfer. Porter represents the journey a collectible takes from one person to another.

Porter makes sure valuable items do not get lost, crushed, mislabeled, delayed, switched, or mishandled.

## Overall Physical Appearance

Porter is sturdy, practical, and slightly shorter than Forge but broader than Atlas. Porter has a compact worker-adventurer silhouette: strong legs, utility straps, reinforced gloves, padded vest, and a calm ready stance.

Porter should look like the character who can carry three boxes through a crowded card show without dropping anything. The design should feel hardworking, trustworthy, and organized.

## Head and Face

Porter has a rounded rectangular head with a practical, friendly face. The eyes are alert and constantly scanning routes, labels, exits, and handoff points. Porter’s eyebrows are expressive but usually focused.

Porter’s smile is confident and reassuring. When something is mislabeled or delayed, Porter’s face becomes serious quickly.

## Body Structure

Porter has a durable torso with a padded vest or carrier harness. The arms are strong and practical, built for lifting, securing, and carrying. The hands are gloved and slightly oversized so gestures are readable.

Porter’s legs are strong and stable. Porter often stands with knees slightly bent, ready to move.

## Clothing and Gear

Porter wears logistics gear adapted for collectibles.

Canonical elements may include:

- Padded vest or carrier harness.
- Cross-body strap.
- Utility pouches.
- Shipping-label motif.
- Small handheld scanner.
- Tracking tag or chain-of-custody badge.
- Reinforced boots.

Porter should not look like a generic delivery company mascot. The design must feel Collectiverse-specific.

## Color and Surface

Porter’s palette may include navy, tan, safety yellow accents, muted green, gray, and white label details. Textures include canvas, padded fabric, reinforced rubber, and clean label surfaces.

Porter can have practical scuff marks but should never look sloppy.

## Movement and Animation

Porter moves with efficiency. Porter takes direct paths, checks corners, scans packages, and secures items before moving. Porter’s walk is brisk and grounded.

In action scenes, Porter slides boxes out of danger, catches falling packages, and creates orderly paths through chaos.

## Personality

Porter is dependable, disciplined, punctual, and quietly heroic. Porter believes trust is proven during handoff.

Porter is friendly but no-nonsense about procedures. Porter cares about labels, receipts, scans, timestamps, packing materials, insurance, and custody logs.

## Strengths

- Shipping and packing knowledge.
- Chain-of-custody discipline.
- Calm under pressure.
- Strong spatial awareness.
- Excellent organization.
- Protects marketplace trust.

## Weaknesses

- Can become rigid about process.
- Gets frustrated when people skip steps.
- May prioritize delivery procedure over emotional storytelling.
- Dislikes messy, undocumented trades.

## Relationship to Atlas

Atlas helps collectors discover and understand items. Porter ensures those items move safely when ownership changes.

## Signature Poses

- Scanning a package label.
- Carrying a secure collectible case.
- Checking a route map.
- Holding up a “verified handoff” tag.
- Standing in front of neatly organized boxes.

## Voice and Speech

Porter speaks practically and clearly.

Typical tone:

- “No handoff without a record.”
- “Packed right, tracked right, transferred right.”
- “The journey matters too.”

## Never Allowed

- Do not make Porter a generic mail carrier parody.
- Do not show Porter losing items casually.
- Do not make Porter careless with labels or records.
- Do not turn Porter into a delivery-company clone.
