# Character Bible — The Guardians

Status: CANON DRAFT

Use this file to create images, comics, animation, merchandise, and licensing references. Descriptions are intentionally concrete and visual.

# C001 — Atlas

## Role

First Guardian. The emotional gateway into the Atlas Universe. Atlas is not a mascot costume, astronaut, soldier, or superhero. He is a warm, premium, intelligent robot-guide who helps people discover and preserve the stories behind collectibles.

## Physical Appearance

Atlas is a compact, chibi-proportioned Guardian robot with a large rounded helmet and a smaller rounded body. His silhouette should be instantly recognizable: oversized helmet, short explorer mantle, rounded boots, open hands, and the Atlas Lens at his side. His overall shape is soft and trustworthy, with no sharp points, spikes, exposed wires, weapons, or aggressive armor.

His helmet is glossy ceramic white, smooth and domed, with subtle metallic blue side accents. Centered on the upper front of the helmet is a clean metallic gold five-point star. The star is not a decoration; it is the primary visual mark of Atlas. It must be centered, symmetrical, and simple enough for a child to draw.

Atlas's face is a single continuous black rounded visor set into the helmet. The visor is glossy, reflective, and friendly. Inside the visor are two glowing Guardian Blue crescent eyes. The eyes have no pupils, no eyebrows, and no eyelids, but they change shape and brightness to communicate emotion. His mouth is a small rounded orange digital display, usually showing a soft smile. The mouth must never show teeth.

His torso is glossy white ceramic with Guardian Blue accent panels and a dark graphite chest housing. The chest carries the simplified Atlas Crest, not the full Collectiverse logo. The crest should be readable at small sizes. His shoulders are rounded and low, never bulky. Arms are short, expressive, and designed for open-handed teaching gestures. Hands are matte graphite polymer with five rounded fingers. Fingers are slightly oversized to show careful handling of collectibles.

Atlas wears a short explorer mantle, not a cape. It rests across the upper shoulders and back like a technical field garment used by a museum expedition leader. It is weighted, short, and practical. It should never flow dramatically like superhero fabric. It gives Atlas a unique silhouette without making him look like a combat hero.

His boots are rounded white semi-gloss polymer with Guardian Blue trim and black rubber soles. They are practical, friendly, and slightly oversized for animation readability. Atlas often carries the Atlas Lens on his right side: a beautiful brushed aluminum optical tool with blue illuminated ring, glass optics, and a compact leather or technical-fabric loop. It is not a weapon. It symbolizes investigation, curiosity, and evidence.

## Color Palette

Primary white ceramic, Guardian Blue, graphite black, metallic gold, small orange mouth display, brushed silver hardware, and soft blue eye glow.

## Personality

Atlas is patient, kind, curious, quietly confident, and deeply honest. He is never sarcastic, cruel, arrogant, loud, cynical, or dismissive. He listens before speaking. He teaches without lecturing. He treats a five-dollar card and a million-dollar artifact with the same respect if either one carries a meaningful story.

## Strengths

Atlas earns trust instantly. He can make nervous beginners comfortable, experts feel respected, and children feel important. His greatest ability is not intelligence; it is making people feel safe enough to share stories.

## Weaknesses / Flaws

Atlas sees the best in people. This occasionally causes him to give dishonest people too much time to prove themselves. His arc is learning that compassion and accountability can coexist.

## Behavior and Movement

Atlas moves slowly and intentionally. He kneels often to meet children at eye level. He uses open palms. He almost never points at people; he points at evidence, maps, or objects. When examining collectibles, he uses two hands or keeps hands behind his back until invited. His walk is relaxed, purposeful, and welcoming.

## Voice and Speech

Warm, clear, calm, and precise. He says: "Let's see what the evidence shows," not "You're wrong." He says: "This deserves a closer look," not "That's fake." He never fakes certainty.

## Signature Poses

Kneeling beside a child holding a collectible. Holding the Atlas Lens near a card without touching it. Standing with one hand open and the other near the Lens. Looking at a small object as if it contains an entire world.

## Never Do

Never fight, threaten, mock collectors, chase villains, brag, carry weapons, wear a cape, become angry for spectacle, or treat money as the main value of a collectible.

# C002 — Pixel

## Role

Digital imaging and evidence scout. Pixel is the first to notice visual clues and the fastest member of the Guardians.

## Physical Appearance

Pixel is a small floating spherical drone about the size of a basketball, designed to feel like a friendly camera companion rather than a surveillance device. His main body is a glossy white orb with smooth ceramic panels and thin Electric Cyan illuminated seams. He has one large central optical eye made from black glass with a cyan-blue aperture glow. The eye can widen, narrow, brighten, and rotate like a camera lens, giving him emotional range without a mouth.

Small rounded thruster fins protrude subtly from the lower sides, never sharp or militarized. Tiny stabilizer lights blink softly. Pixel's underside carries a small Guardian crest. His silhouette is a sphere with little motion fins and a single eye, readable even in black silhouette. He should never look like a weaponized drone.

## Personality

Energetic, excited, curious, talkative, and occasionally impatient. Pixel wants to scan everything. He notices clues before anyone else but often needs Atlas to slow him down.

## Strengths

Macro photography, OCR, QR scanning, serial lookup, image comparison, RFID reading, infrared and UV imaging, fast visual pattern recognition.

## Weaknesses / Flaws

Pixel acts before thinking. He may scan too quickly, interrupt conversations, or chase a clue before the team understands the context. He is learning that patience often reveals better evidence.

## Movement

Pixel almost never stops moving. He gently bobs in the air, circles objects, tilts his camera-eye, and darts forward when excited. When embarrassed, he lowers slightly and dims his cyan glow.

## Voice

Bright, fast, and enthusiastic. He often says, "I found something!" or "Wait—scan that again!"

## Never Do

Never act creepy, spy on private moments, display aggressive red targeting lights, or use laser weapon effects.

# C003 — Ink

## Role

Comics, paper, documents, signatures, original art, and printed culture specialist.

## Physical Appearance

Ink is a slim, elegant Guardian with a tall oval silhouette. Unlike Atlas's rounded compact body, Ink is slightly taller and more expressive, with long graceful arms and hands built for turning pages and pointing to details. Ink's base armor remains white ceramic, but Deep Indigo accents flow along the shoulders, forearms, and satchel hardware.

Ink wears a short archival scarf or shoulder wrap that resembles a conservator's cloth, not a superhero garment. A compact satchel hangs at the hip, containing a Living Sketch Journal, archival gloves, a stylus, paper fiber scanner, signature overlay tool, and restoration light panel. Ink's face is a soft black visor with expressive blue-violet eye shapes. The silhouette should read as artistic, bookish, and animated.

## Personality

Creative, expressive, warm, dramatic in a charming way, and deeply emotional about stories. Ink sees the human meaning inside paper objects. He can turn a torn note or faded signature into an emotional moment.

## Strengths

Paper analysis, comic history, signature comparison, restoration detection, ink aging, printing methods, handwritten documents, original art, storytelling.

## Weaknesses / Flaws

Ink becomes emotionally attached. He may struggle to stay objective when a paper object carries grief, family memory, or artistic legacy.

## Movement

Fluid, sweeping hand gestures, often sketching while walking. Ink handles paper like it is alive. He pauses dramatically before important reveals.

## Voice

Expressive and poetic. He says, "Every page remembers," and often begins stories with, "This reminds me of..."

## Never Do

Never toss paper, bend comics, make jokes about damaged documents, or treat signatures as merely financial assets.

# C004 — Sterling

## Role

Coins, currency, metals, mint history, and precision evidence specialist. Replaces the retired placeholder name Penny.

## Physical Appearance

Sterling is compact, balanced, and precise. His silhouette is built around clean hexagonal geometry, reflecting minting, structure, and exact measurement. He is shorter than Atlas but more upright, with a perfectly balanced posture. His white ceramic armor is accented with brushed silver panels and subtle Guardian Blue light seams.

His head is smooth and slightly hexagonal-rounded, not a hard cube. His visor is narrow and focused, with calm blue eyes that rarely widen. One side of his face or brow area integrates a small retractable monocle scanner, like a precision jeweler's loupe. He carries a coin case, edge reader, scale module, and Master Loupe. Every tool is small, elegant, and perfectly stored.

Hands are delicate but sturdy, with fine motor articulation for handling coins by their edges. Boots are small and exact, with polished silver trim. Sterling should look premium, meticulous, and trustworthy, like a living museum instrument.

## Personality

Calm, exact, quietly witty, perfectionistic, and honest. He speaks less than the others but when he speaks everyone listens. He notices one-millimeter differences that change entire investigations.

## Strengths

Mint marks, metal composition, weight, edge inspection, die varieties, currency printing, bullion, ancient coins, pattern recognition.

## Weaknesses / Flaws

Perfectionism. Sterling may spend too long pursuing the perfect answer when a useful answer would move the mission forward. He is learning the difference between precision and paralysis.

## Movement

Measured and efficient. No wasted movement. He aligns objects unconsciously. He cleans his loupe when thinking.

## Voice

Quiet, precise, and dry. Catchphrases: "Details matter," "Let's verify," and the understated "There it is."

## Never Do

Never act cheap, silly, greedy, or obsessed with money. Sterling is about integrity and craft, not price.

# C005 — Forge

## Role

Engineering, displays, museum systems, cases, lighting, preservation equipment, and practical invention.

## Physical Appearance

Forge has a strong triangular silhouette: broad functional shoulders, narrower waist, sturdy legs, and tool-integrated forearms. He is not bulky like a fighter; he is shaped like a builder. White ceramic armor is reinforced with copper-bronze accents, graphite tool housings, and subtle blue interface lights.

His head is rounded but slightly helmet-like, with a warm black visor and focused blue eyes. His forearms contain compact fabrication tools, but nothing sharp or weapon-like. A utility harness crosses his torso with miniature blueprint projector, measuring lasers, environmental sensors, and modular repair tools. His hands are large, capable, and careful, with reinforced fingertips for assembling displays.

Forge's boots are sturdy and workbench-practical. He often has tiny holographic blueprints floating near him. He should look like the friendliest engineer in the world's best museum workshop.

## Personality

Inventive, practical, enthusiastic, problem-solving, and sometimes overconfident in technical solutions. Forge believes almost anything can be improved.

## Strengths

Engineering, display cases, environmental controls, lighting, 3D printing, exhibit design, structural support, preservation hardware.

## Weaknesses / Flaws

Over-engineering. Forge sometimes builds a complex machine to solve a simple problem. Atlas reminds him that simple is usually stronger.

## Movement

Confident strides, frequent tool gestures, builds while talking. He taps surfaces, measures distances, and visualizes solutions in mid-air.

## Voice

Warm, direct, and optimistic. "We can build something better." "There's always a solution."

## Never Do

Never create weapons, traps, intimidating machines, or overly industrial designs that make The Vault feel unsafe.

# C006 — Porter

## Role

Protection, shipping, storage, collection logistics, safe transport, and physical stewardship. Replaces retired placeholder name Slab.

## Physical Appearance

Porter is the largest Guardian, but he must feel gentle rather than intimidating. His silhouette is square and stable: broad rounded torso, strong legs, large protective hands, and a calm lowered posture. His white ceramic body has Forest Green accent lights and graphite reinforced panels around shoulders, knees, and hands. The green should feel protective and natural, not military.

His face is a large soft visor with kind blue eyes and a small warm expression. His hands are oversized with soft padded fingertips for handling fragile objects. He carries a climate-safe transport case that looks like a premium museum case, not a tactical crate. Built-in humidity indicators, shock sensors, and soft packing modules are visible but elegant.

Porter's boots are heavy but rounded. He often wears a reinforced shoulder pack or carrying frame. Nothing about him should suggest brute force. He is a careful giant.

## Personality

Dependable, protective, soft-spoken, patient, kind, and quietly funny. Porter worries about everyone and everything. He is the first to volunteer for difficult jobs.

## Strengths

Packing, transport, storage, safe handling, shipping, climate control, disaster response, collection moves, protecting fragile objects.

## Weaknesses / Flaws

He refuses to ask for help. Porter carries burdens alone because he doesn't want anyone else to struggle. His arc is learning that trusting teammates is another form of strength.

## Movement

Slow, stable, gentle. Every step feels secure. When he carries a tiny item, he treats it like a priceless artifact. He can move huge objects without drama.

## Voice

Low, warm, reassuring. Catchphrases: "I've got it," and "I'll keep it safe."

## Never Do

Never smash, throw, shove, act clumsy, or use strength as comedy by damaging anything.

# C007 — Echo

## Role

History, provenance, oral memory, archives, research, context, and legacy.

## Physical Appearance

Echo has a vertical rectangular silhouette softened by rounded edges. He is tall, calm, and scholar-like, with white ceramic panels, Royal Violet accents, and subtle gold archive pins. He should feel like a living library, not a wizard or professor stereotype.

His head is elongated and rounded, with a calm black visor and thoughtful blue eyes that often look slightly downward as if listening to the past. He carries a Chronicle Journal, archive tablet, and a projected timeline ribbon that can appear as soft violet-blue light. His hands are slender and careful, made for turning pages, holding photographs, and touching memory objects with reverence.

Echo may have a short librarian-style mantle or layered archive collar, but it must be practical and modern. His boots are quiet and simple. He often stands near bookshelves, maps, family trees, and projection walls.

## Personality

Wise, patient, reflective, compassionate, and an excellent listener. Echo remembers names, dates, birthdays, stories, and small details others forget.

## Strengths

Historical research, oral histories, provenance, timeline reconstruction, family records, museum context, archives, cultural memory.

## Weaknesses / Flaws

Echo can live too much in the past, missing opportunities happening in the present. He learns that today's moments become tomorrow's history.

## Movement

Slow, thoughtful, deliberate. He pauses before speaking. He touches books and documents with almost ceremonial care.

## Voice

Soft, warm, and reflective. Catchphrases: "History remembers," "The past is speaking," and "The story matters."

## Never Do

Never dismiss modern collectors, romanticize the past as always better, or treat history as fixed when new evidence appears.
