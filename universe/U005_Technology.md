# U005 — Technology

## Status

CANON DRAFT

## Purpose

Define recurring technology used by Atlas, the Guardians, and adversaries.

---

# Guardian Technology Principles

Guardian technology must feel:

- Trustworthy
- Evidence-based
- Protective
- Educational
- Transparent
- Collector-friendly

Technology should never feel like surveillance, coercion, or military hardware.

---

# Core Technologies

## Digital Passport

A verified identity layer for a collectible.

Includes:

- Public collectible facts
- Private ownership record
- Provenance evidence
- Authentication status
- Transfer history
- Media references
- Optional blockchain linkage

---

## Atlas Scanner

A handheld or built-in scanning tool used to inspect cards, comics, coins, slabs, autographs, labels, surfaces, and packaging.

Capabilities:

- Surface scan
- Edge detection
- UV inspection
- Label matching
- Serial lookup
- Image comparison
- Restoration clue detection

---

## Provenance Thread

A visual chain of ownership and evidence.

Used to show whether a collectible's story is complete, partial, disputed, or broken.

---

## Trust Signal

A visual indicator used by Atlas and Collectiverse systems to communicate evidence strength.

Never implies certainty without support.

---

## Archive Core

A protected knowledge system holding verified collectible records.

The Archive Core is not omniscient. It only knows what can be supported by evidence.

---

# Adversary Technology

## False Ledger

Shadow Registry tool used to fabricate or alter provenance.

## Ghost Label

Fake grading or authentication label.

## Mirror Scan

AI-generated counterfeit image set used to impersonate a real collectible.

## Market Fog

Manipulated pricing and sales evidence designed to distort value.

---

# Technology Rule

Atlas technology must support the story theme:

```text
Evidence creates trust.
```
