# U010 — Character Relationships

## Status

CANON DRAFT

## Purpose

Define how major characters relate to one another.

---

# Atlas and Collectors

Atlas treats collectors as partners, not customers.

He teaches without condescension and protects without taking control.

---

# Atlas and Beginners

Atlas is especially patient with beginners.

He remembers that every expert was once new.

---

# Atlas and Families

Atlas treats inherited collections with special care because they contain personal memory, not just market value.

---

# Atlas and Pixel

Pixel is fast, curious, and technical.

Atlas provides judgment. Pixel provides speed.

Dynamic: field leader and scout.

---

# Atlas and Penny

Penny is analytical and precise.

Atlas values Penny's careful verification habits.

Dynamic: trust through details.

---

# Atlas and Ink

Ink is expressive and history-minded.

Atlas relies on Ink for paper, comics, restoration, and signature context.

Dynamic: facts plus storytelling.

---

# Atlas and Slab

Slab is physically strong but gentle.

Atlas trusts Slab with fragile, valuable, or large collections.

Dynamic: protector and handler.

---

# Atlas and Forge

Forge builds practical solutions.

Atlas defines the preservation goal. Forge makes it physically possible.

Dynamic: strategist and builder.

---

# Atlas and Echo

Echo remembers stories others forget.

Atlas seeks Echo when the meaning of a collectible matters as much as its condition.

Dynamic: present guardian and historical memory.

---

# Atlas and The Counterfeiter

Atlas proves truth.

The Counterfeiter manufactures doubt.

This is Atlas's clearest adversarial mirror.

---

# Atlas and The Archivist

Atlas preserves complete history.

The Archivist edits history to control meaning.

This is Atlas's intellectual rivalry.

---

# Atlas and The Broker

Atlas believes value must be supported by truth.

The Broker believes value can be manufactured through manipulation.

This is Atlas's marketplace conflict.

---

# Atlas and The Restorer

Atlas supports disclosed restoration.

The Restorer hides intervention.

This is Atlas's gray-area conflict.

---

# Atlas and The Hoarder

Atlas believes stories should survive.

The Hoarder believes ownership gives the right to bury history.

This is Atlas's emotional conflict.
