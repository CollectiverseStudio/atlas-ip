# U001 — The Guardians

## Status

CANON DRAFT

## Summary

The Guardians are the central protective organization in the Collectiverse universe. They are not police, soldiers, superheroes, or bounty hunters. They are stewards of collectible history.

Their purpose is to protect truth, provenance, memory, and ownership.

---

## Mission

```text
Discover.
Authenticate.
Protect.
Preserve.
Connect.
```

---

## Core Belief

Every collectible has a story.

Every story deserves truth.

---

## Foundational Principle

A Guardian never certifies what cannot be verified.

This is not a weakness. It is the moral foundation of the organization.

---

## Atlas

Atlas is the First Guardian.

He is the public face, field leader, guide, teacher, and moral center of the Collectiverse.

Atlas does not command through authority. He leads through trust.

---

## Guardian Divisions

### Authentication Division

Verifies collectibles, signatures, slabs, provenance, catalog records, digital passports, and ownership history.

### Preservation Division

Protects physical items from light, humidity, mishandling, deterioration, and improper storage.

### Archive Division

Maintains historical records, ownership chains, catalog entries, digital passports, and provenance evidence.

### Marketplace Integrity Division

Detects scams, manipulated listings, shill activity, counterfeit items, fake slabs, and seller trust issues.

### Expedition Division

Finds lost collections, forgotten attic discoveries, estate collections, misplaced records, and historically significant collectibles.

### Research Division

Studies collectible history, manufacturers, sets, printing variations, cultural context, and rarity patterns.

### Legacy Division

Helps families preserve inherited collections and transfer their stories responsibly.

---

## Guardian Code

1. Never certify without evidence.
2. Never rewrite history for profit.
3. Never value money above truth.
4. Always protect the collector and the collectible.
5. Preserve provenance whenever possible.
6. Respect rightful ownership.
7. Teach before judging.
8. Protect beginners from exploitation.
9. Admit uncertainty clearly.
10. Keep the story alive.

---

## Visual Identity

The Guardians use clean white, deep blue, dark graphite, gold, and silver.

Their symbols must feel trustworthy, archival, technical, and timeless.

---

## Story Role

The Guardians allow Collectiverse product areas to become part of the universe:

- Passport becomes provenance protection.
- Marketplace becomes trust enforcement.
- Live becomes public discovery.
- Events become field operations.
- Legacy becomes inheritance preservation.
- Inventory becomes private archive management.
