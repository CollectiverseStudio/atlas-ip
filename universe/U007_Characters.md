# U007 — Characters

## Status

CANON DRAFT

## Purpose

Define the initial character roster for the Collectiverse universe.

---

# Atlas

## Role

First Guardian of Collectibles.

## Identity

Trusted guide, educator, protector, and investigator.

## Core Traits

- Honest
- Curious
- Patient
- Warm
- Evidence-driven
- Protective
- Optimistic

## Moral Rule

Atlas never certifies what he cannot verify.

## Mission Constraint

Atlas cannot preserve what people choose to discard.

---

# Guardian Specialists

## Pixel

Small floating scanning drone.

Specializes in:

- Photography
- Scanning
- OCR
- QR codes
- RFID
- Image comparison
- Fast field inspection

Personality: quick, energetic, precise.

---

## Penny

Small coin-focused analysis bot.

Specializes in:

- Coins
- Currency
- Bullion
- Metal detection
- Mint marks
- Ancient coin references

Personality: analytical, detail-obsessed, quietly witty.

---

## Ink

Comic and paper collectible expert.

Specializes in:

- Comics
- Signatures
- Restoration
- Paper condition
- Printing variants
- Creator history

Personality: artistic, expressive, highly knowledgeable.

---

## Slab

Large, gentle preservation and logistics robot.

Specializes in:

- Shipping
- Packaging
- Vaulting
- Storage
- Moving large collections
- Damage prevention

Personality: strong, calm, careful, deeply kind.

---

## Forge

Build and display systems specialist.

Specializes in:

- Cases
- Shelving
- Lighting
- Display rooms
- Collection environments
- Safe presentation

Personality: practical, inventive, hands-on.

---

## Echo

Historian and lore specialist.

Specializes in:

- Sports history
- Pop culture
- Film props
- Autographs
- Provenance stories
- Historical context

Personality: wise, narrative-driven, reflective.
