# Collectiverse Universe Bible

## Status

ACTIVE

## Purpose

This folder defines the story world around Atlas, the Guardians, their adversaries, locations, technology, symbols, and long-term narrative canon.

Atlas is no longer treated as a standalone mascot. Atlas is the lead character in the Collectiverse universe.

---

## Core Premise

Collectibles are not just objects. They are containers of memory, provenance, identity, culture, and history.

The Collectiverse exists to discover, authenticate, protect, preserve, and connect those stories.

Atlas is the first Guardian of Collectibles.
