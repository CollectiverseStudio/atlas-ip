## Status

CANON DRAFT

## Purpose

Define recurring locations in the Collectiverse universe.

---

# The Vault

## Type

Guardian headquarters.

## Description

The Vault is not a bank vault. It is a living archive, research center, authentication lab, preservation facility, and mission hub.

It is clean, warm, futuristic, trustworthy, and built around the idea that every object has a story worth protecting.

## Key Areas

- Atlas Command Center
- Authentication Lab
- Preservation Lab
- Digital Passport Center
- Marketplace Integrity Operations
- Hall of Collections
- Historical Library
- Restoration Analysis Lab
- Archive Core
- Expedition Map Room

---

# Hall of Collections

A museum-like space inside The Vault showing representative collectibles across eras and categories.

It is not a trophy room. It is a teaching space.

---

# Authentication Lab

A clean, technical space with microscopes, UV lights, scanners, spectrometers, slab readers, and comparison databases.

Used for educational shorts and mystery stories.

---

# Preservation Lab

A controlled-environment room for safe handling, storage, humidity analysis, light exposure testing, and conservation guidance.

---

# Digital Passport Center

Where physical items are linked to digital passports, ownership history, provenance records, and verification pages.

---

# The Archive Core

The most protected part of The Vault.

Stores evidence, provenance chains, catalog history, and verified collectible knowledge.

Story note: The Shadow Registry wants access to this.
