# U003 — Organizations

## Status

CANON DRAFT

## Purpose

Define the major organizations inside the Collectiverse universe.

---

# The Guardians

## Role

Primary protagonist organization.

## Public Perception

Trusted guides, educators, authenticators, protectors, and archivists.

## Actual Function

A distributed network of specialists who protect collectible truth across physical, digital, and historical systems.

## Operating Principle

Truth before value.

---

# The Shadow Registry

## Role

Primary adversarial network.

## Public Perception

Often invisible. Members may appear as dealers, archivists, investors, restorers, collectors, appraisers, or marketplace operators.

## Actual Function

A hidden network that profits from confusion, controlled records, forged provenance, and market distortion.

## Operating Principle

Control the record, control the value.

---

# Independent Collectors

## Role

The heart of the universe.

Collectors may be beginners, lifelong hobbyists, estate inheritors, dealers, kids, families, historians, or investors.

Atlas serves them first.

---

# Trusted Houses

## Role

Auction houses, grading partners, museums, appraisers, estate managers, shops, and community leaders that cooperate with the Guardians.

## Story Use

Trusted Houses provide leads, evidence, friction, and field locations.

---

# Gray Market Rings

## Role

Not always part of the Shadow Registry, but often exploited by it.

## Activities

- Undisclosed restoration.
- Raw-card bait listings.
- Stolen scans.
- Fake slabs.
- Shill bidding.
- Laundered provenance.

---

# The Lost Archive

## Role

A rumored collection of missing provenance records, lost catalogs, private ledgers, factory proofs, artist notes, signing records, print sheets, and ownership chains.

## Story Function

The Lost Archive can drive multi-episode story arcs.

Both the Guardians and the Shadow Registry want access.
