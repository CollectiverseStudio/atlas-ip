# U008 — Gadgets

## Status

CANON DRAFT

## Purpose

Define the tools Atlas and the Guardians use.

---

# Gadget Design Rule

Guardian tools must be friendly, safe, educational, and evidence-focused.

They are not weapons.

---

# Atlas Toolkit

## UV Beacon

Portable UV light used to inspect restoration, surface alteration, inks, labels, and hidden marks.

## Provenance Scanner

Scans documents, labels, serials, QR codes, signatures, and ownership evidence.

## Loupe Lens

Magnification device for surface detail, print dots, paper fibers, ink breaks, and card edges.

## Passport Pad

Tablet-like device used to link an item to a Digital Passport.

## Archive Key

Symbolic and functional device that grants Atlas access to verified archive records.

## Field Case

Compact protective case containing sleeves, gloves, team bags, scanner tools, microfiber cloths, and reference cards.
