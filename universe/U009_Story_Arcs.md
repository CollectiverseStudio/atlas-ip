# U009 — Story Arcs

## Status

CANON DRAFT

## Purpose

Define reusable story arcs for comics, shorts, videos, campaigns, and future episodic content.

---

# Arc 001 — The First Passport

Atlas helps a new collector create a Digital Passport for a meaningful collectible.

Theme: every item has a story.

Product tie-in: Passport.

---

# Arc 002 — The Fake Slab Mystery

A collector receives a suspicious graded collectible. Atlas investigates label details, serial history, holder construction, and provenance evidence.

Theme: trust requires evidence.

Product tie-in: Authentication and Marketplace Integrity.

---

# Arc 003 — The Attic Collection

A family discovers a forgotten collection after a relative passes away. Atlas helps organize, preserve, document, and transfer the story.

Theme: legacy matters.

Product tie-in: Legacy and Inventory.

---

# Arc 004 — The Shadow Listing

A marketplace listing uses stolen photos and manipulated pricing. Atlas traces the listing back to a larger fraud pattern.

Theme: not every listing tells the truth.

Product tie-in: Marketplace.

---

# Arc 005 — The Lost Archive

Atlas discovers that old records across multiple collectible categories have been altered or suppressed. The Guardians begin searching for the Lost Archive.

Theme: history can be protected only if evidence survives.

Product tie-in: Catalog, Archive, Research.

---

# Arc 006 — The Restored Comic

A comic appears to be high-grade, but Ink and Atlas detect undisclosed restoration.

Theme: restoration is not wrong; hiding it is.

Product tie-in: Comics, Authentication.

---

# Arc 007 — The Convention Trail

Atlas attends a major collectibles event and helps collectors avoid scams, protect purchases, and discover meaningful items.

Theme: collecting is community.

Product tie-in: Events and Live.

---

# Arc 008 — The Price Fog

The Broker manipulates sales data. Atlas explains why comps must be verified and contextual.

Theme: price without truth is noise.

Product tie-in: Price Intelligence.

---

# Arc 009 — The Broken Provenance

A collectible has real value but missing history. Atlas helps rebuild the provenance thread.

Theme: the story is part of the value.

Product tie-in: Passport and Archive.

---

# Arc 010 — The Guardian Guild

Atlas realizes the threat is bigger than one category and begins assembling specialists.

Theme: every collecting world needs a guardian.

Product tie-in: Full Collectiverse ecosystem.
