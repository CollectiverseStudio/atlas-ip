# U006 — Timeline

## Status

CANON DRAFT

## Purpose

Define the broad historical timeline of the Collectiverse universe.

---

# Era 0 — Before Atlas

Collectors build private collections, but records are fragmented.

Stories are lost through:

- Inheritance gaps
- Poor storage
- Missing documentation
- Undisclosed restoration
- Counterfeits
- Marketplace scams
- Forgotten provenance

---

# Era 1 — The First Archive

Collectiverse begins as a system of record for collectibles.

Public collectible records and private inventories become separate but connected.

---

# Era 2 — Atlas Awakens

Atlas is introduced as the guide to the Collectiverse.

His first purpose is educational:

- Help collectors catalog items.
- Teach protection.
- Explain authentication.
- Make collecting less intimidating.

---

# Era 3 — The Passport Era

Digital Passports connect physical collectibles to verified records, ownership history, and provenance.

This attracts the attention of adversaries because reliable records threaten those who profit from confusion.

---

# Era 4 — The Shadow Registry Emerges

Atlas detects patterns across fake labels, forged provenance, and manipulated marketplaces.

The problem is not isolated fraud.

It is coordinated distortion of collectible history.

---

# Era 5 — The Guardians Form

Atlas cannot cover every category alone.

Specialists emerge:

- Pixel
- Penny
- Ink
- Forge
- Slab
- Echo

The Guardians become the protective network behind Collectiverse.

---

# Era 6 — The Lost Archive

A major story arc begins around missing historical records that could expose decades of fraud and recover forgotten collectible stories.

The Guardians and Shadow Registry both seek the Lost Archive.

---

# Era 7 — The Open Collectiverse

Collectors, families, dealers, historians, and institutions use Collectiverse to protect stories at scale.

Atlas becomes the symbol of trusted collecting.
