# U002 — Adversaries

## Status

CANON DRAFT

## Summary

Atlas's adversaries are not generic villains. They represent real threats to collectors, collectible history, provenance, authenticity, ownership, and market trust.

They are not defined by physical strength. They are defined by opposition to truth.

---

## Opposing Philosophy

Atlas believes collectibles connect people to truth.

The adversaries believe collectibles are tools for control, profit, manipulation, status, or deception.

---

## Primary Adversary Network

### Working Name

The Shadow Registry

### Purpose

The Shadow Registry profits from confusion.

It manipulates provenance, counterfeits collectibles, distorts markets, steals ownership records, and rewrites history for advantage.

### Core Belief

```text
History belongs to those who control the record.
```

---

## Adversary Types

### 001 — The Counterfeiter

Master of fake cards, fake autographs, fake slabs, fake labels, fake certificates, and manufactured provenance.

Opposes: Authentication Division

Atlas conflict: Atlas proves truth; the Counterfeiter manufactures doubt.

---

### 002 — The Broker

Manipulates market demand, shill bidding, artificial scarcity, hidden reserves, and inflated comps.

Opposes: Marketplace Integrity Division

Atlas conflict: Atlas protects fair value; the Broker weaponizes price.

---

### 003 — The Smuggler

Moves stolen collections, illicit artifacts, missing heirlooms, and hidden estate assets.

Opposes: Expedition and Legacy Divisions

Atlas conflict: Atlas restores rightful ownership; the Smuggler hides the trail.

---

### 004 — The Restorer

A gray-area adversary who believes undisclosed restoration is acceptable if the final object looks better.

Opposes: Preservation Division

Atlas conflict: Atlas supports restoration only when disclosed; the Restorer hides intervention.

---

### 005 — The Hacker

Targets digital passports, ownership records, wallets, private inventories, authentication records, and marketplace trust systems.

Opposes: Archive and Passport systems

Atlas conflict: Atlas protects digital truth; the Hacker corrupts evidence.

---

### 006 — The Archivist

A corrupted historian who selectively edits, suppresses, or rewrites provenance to control historical meaning.

Opposes: Archive and Research Divisions

Atlas conflict: Atlas preserves the full story; the Archivist curates only convenient truth.

---

### 007 — The Hoarder

Locks historically meaningful collectibles away forever and refuses documentation, sharing, education, or responsible succession.

Opposes: Legacy and Research Divisions

Atlas conflict: Atlas believes stories should survive beyond one owner; the Hoarder believes ownership means silence.

---

## Naming Rule

These are role names, not final trademark names.

Final character names must be cleared for uniqueness before being locked.

Avoid generic names as final marks when possible.

---

## Tone Rule

Adversaries may be dangerous, deceptive, or manipulative, but the Collectiverse universe should remain suitable for a broad collector audience.

Avoid graphic violence, cruelty, horror, or political framing.
