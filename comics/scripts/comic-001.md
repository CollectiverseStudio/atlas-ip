# Comic 001: Rookie Cards Explained
Layout: grid
Title: Atlas Tips #001: Rookie Cards

## Panel 1
Prompt: atlas_character standing in the entrance of a warm cozy card shop, arms spread wide in welcome, golden afternoon light streaming through the front windows, shelves packed with sports cards and memorabilia behind him, excited welcoming expression with bright green eyes glowing
Text: Hey collectors! Welcome back! Today we're diving deep into one of the MOST important topics in card collecting — ROOKIE CARDS!
Bubble: top-left

## Panel 2
Prompt: atlas_character holding up a single basketball card with both hands, the card glowing with a soft golden aura, close-up shot showing his enthusiastic face behind the card, card shop display cases in soft focus background, warm overhead lighting
Text: A rookie card is a player's FIRST officially licensed trading card — printed during their first year of professional play. It's where legends begin!
Bubble: top-right

## Panel 3
Prompt: atlas_character in professor pose with one finger raised, a holographic timeline floating beside him showing a player's journey from draft day to first card, educational setting with card reference books on a desk, soft blue informational lighting
Text: But here's what trips up new collectors — not every card from a player's first year is technically a "rookie card." There are RULES!
Bubble: top-left

## Panel 4
Prompt: atlas_character pointing at a large magnified RC logo floating in the air as a hologram, the logo glowing bright gold, card shop background with other collectors browsing in soft focus, confident teaching expression
Text: Modern sets make it easy — look for the RC logo! That little badge means the card is OFFICIALLY designated as a rookie by the manufacturer.
Bubble: top-right

## Panel 5
Prompt: atlas_character holding two cards side by side for comparison, one from a base set and one from a premium set, both with RC logos, holographic labels floating above each card identifying the set names, warm display lighting in a collector's study
Text: A player can have MULTIPLE rookie cards from different sets in the same year! Topps, Panini, Upper Deck — each company prints their own version.
Bubble: top-left

## Panel 6
Prompt: atlas_character examining a vintage baseball card with a magnifying glass, the card showing age and patina, sepia-toned nostalgic lighting, old wooden card shop backdrop with vintage posters, curious investigative expression
Text: For vintage cards there's no RC logo — you just need to know which year was the player's FIRST appearance. Research is your superpower here!
Bubble: top-right

## Panel 7
Prompt: atlas_character standing next to a floating holographic chart showing card values increasing over time, an arrow pointing upward dramatically, golden sparkle effects around the peak, modern tech-style background with data visualizations, impressed wide-eyed expression
Text: Why do rookies matter? Because they capture the BEGINNING of greatness! A player's rookie card almost always becomes their most valuable card.
Bubble: top-left

## Panel 8
Prompt: atlas_character holding up a graded PSA 10 rookie card slab with reverent care, divine golden rays emanating from the slab, sparkle and star effects surrounding it, dramatic upward lighting on his face, awestruck expression with mouth slightly open
Text: A gem mint rookie of a superstar player? That's the HOLY GRAIL of card collecting. Some sell for millions! The condition matters just as much as the player.
Bubble: top-right

## Panel 9
Prompt: atlas_character crouching next to a dollar bin at a card show, pulling out a card with a surprised excited expression, other collectors walking past in the background unaware, dramatic spotlight effect on the discovered card, convention hall setting
