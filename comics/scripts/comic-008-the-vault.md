## Production Notes
- Color palette: Deep blues, golden vault lighting, reinforced steel grays, warm interior golds
- Porter's design: LARGE (biggest Keeper), armor-like uniform, vault-door aesthetic details
- The Vault should feel GRAND — cathedral of collecting, not just a safe
- Items in cases should be recognizable to collectors but stylized
- The vault represents the aspirational end of collecting — treat everything with care
- Porter speaks economically — few words, each one counts
- This establishes KEY lore: The Keepers maintain The Vault to protect legendary items
- Scale contrast: Porter is HUGE next to the vault door, Atlas looks small = the vault is MASSIVE
- Thread format on Twitter: Build suspense (door opening, reveal, full scope, farewell)
- Alt text: "Porter the Vault Keeper shows Atlas and viewers the legendary collection vault"
