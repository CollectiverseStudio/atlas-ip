## Production Notes
- Color palette: Warm golds, magical sparkles, cozy hobby shop tones
- The kid character should be designed to be relatable/universal (no specific ethnicity dominant, could represent anyone)
- Atlas appears in Panel 2 — builds the "magical guide" narrative
- Hobby shop should feel warm and inviting (not sterile or intimidating)
- Card designs should be generic but exciting-looking
- Engagement hook: Final panel asks audience to share their story
- Consider this as a "pilot" for the Collector Stories series — kid could recur
- Alt text: "A young collector opens their first pack of cards with Atlas the Keeper guiding them"
