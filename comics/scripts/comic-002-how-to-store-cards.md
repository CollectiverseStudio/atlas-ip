# Comic 002: How to Store Cards Properly
Layout: grid
Title: Atlas Tips #002: Card Storage 101

## Panel 1
Prompt: atlas_character looking horrified with hands on cheeks, staring down at a bent damaged sports card on a dusty table, dramatic harsh lighting casting dark shadows over the damaged card, collector's workshop background with storage supplies on shelves, comedic overreaction pose
Text: NOOO! A card left unprotected is a card in DANGER! This poor thing never stood a chance. Let me show you how to NEVER let this happen!
Bubble: top-left

## Panel 2
Prompt: atlas_character holding up a clear penny sleeve between two fingers like a precious artifact, the sleeve catching light with a soft protective blue glow, workshop background with organized supplies, calm reassuring expression, educational lighting
Text: Step 1 — the PENNY SLEEVE! This soft plastic sleeve is your card's first layer of armor. They cost about one cent each — the cheapest insurance in the hobby!
Bubble: top-right

## Panel 3
Prompt: atlas_character carefully sliding a card into a penny sleeve with delicate precise movements, extreme close-up showing his gentle hands and concentrated expression, tongue slightly out in focus, soft workshop lighting, the card gliding smoothly into the sleeve
Text: Slide it in gently — top to bottom, no bending, no forcing. Treat every card like it could be worth a thousand dollars someday. Because it might be!
Bubble: top-left

## Panel 4
Prompt: atlas_character holding a rigid toploader in one hand and the sleeved card in the other, about to combine them, the toploader glowing with a protective shield effect, workshop background with a step-by-step diagram on the wall, confident teaching expression
Text: Step 2 — the TOPLOADER! This rigid plastic shield stops bends, dings, and corner damage. Your card is leveling up its defense!
Bubble: top-right

## Panel 5
Prompt: atlas_character sliding the penny-sleeved card down into the toploader, satisfying click effect shown with small sparkle at the top, side-angle view showing the snug fit, workshop lighting with warm overhead glow, satisfied smile forming
Text: Penny sleeve goes IN the toploader — never bare card into a toploader! The soft sleeve prevents scratching against the hard plastic. Double protection!
Bubble: top-left

## Panel 6
Prompt: atlas_character standing next to a neat white storage box, placing the toploaded card upright inside alongside other organized cards, the box showing rows of perfectly stored cards, clean organized workshop, proud methodical expression, soft warm lighting
Text: Step 3 — the STORAGE BOX! Cards stored upright, organized by set or player. Now your cards have THREE layers of protection. That's the Keeper way!
Bubble: top-right

## Panel 7
Prompt: atlas_character pointing at a wall-mounted thermometer and humidity gauge, both showing ideal ranges highlighted in green, a climate-controlled room behind him with dehumidifier visible, serious educational expression, blue informational lighting
Text: ENVIRONMENT matters! Keep cards away from direct sunlight, extreme heat, and humidity. Aim for 65-70°F and low moisture. Your cards will thank you!
Bubble: top-left

## Panel 8
Prompt: atlas_character holding up a magnetic one-touch case with a beautiful card inside, the case gleaming under display lighting, shelving unit with premium displays behind him, impressed appreciative expression, warm golden spotlight on the case
Text: For your BEST cards? Upgrade to magnetic one-touch holders or graded slabs! These are for the stars of your collection that deserve to shine.
Bubble: top-right

## Panel 9
Prompt: atlas_character wagging a finger in a friendly warning, red X symbols floating around bad examples — a card in a rubber band, a card in direct sunlight, a card in a shoebox, humorous cautionary lighting with dramatic red accents
Text: Quick DON'Ts — never use rubber bands, never stack loose cards, and NEVER store them in attics or garages! Heat and moisture are the enemy!
Bubble: top-left

## Panel 10
Prompt: atlas_character arms crossed proudly in front of a perfectly organized card collection on shelves behind him, everything neat and protected, warm golden lighting making the collection glow, triumphant confident smile, looking directly at viewer
Text: Penny sleeve, toploader, storage box — that's the formula! Protect your collection today so it can grow in value tomorrow. Now go organize those cards!
