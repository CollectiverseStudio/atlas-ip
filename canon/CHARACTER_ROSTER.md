## DESIGN RULES (Universal)

### Style

- Pixar/DreamWorks quality 3D CGI render
- Chibi proportions (big head, small body)
- Rounded forms, soft lighting
- Plain white background (for LoRA training)
- Full body, centered, square (1024×1024)
- NO text, NO other characters, NO complex backgrounds

### Heroes vs Villains

| Trait | Keepers (Heroes) | Syndicate (Villains) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Palette | Blue/white/gold | Red/black/dark + signature color |
| Forms | Rounded, friendly | Sharper angles within rounded forms |
| Badge | Glowing blue hexagon with gold "C" | Corrupted/distorted hexagons |
| Energy | Guardians of the Galaxy | Syndrome/Lotso/Doofenshmirtz |
| Tone | Brave, scrappy, fun | Clever, theatrical, NOT scary |

### LoRA Caption Format

- Heroes: `{name}_character, a {size} cute chibi {species} with {key features}, blue hexagon badge with gold C on chest, Pixar CGI style`
- Villains: `{name}_character, a {size} chibi {species} with {key features}, {badge description}, villain design, Pixar CGI style`
