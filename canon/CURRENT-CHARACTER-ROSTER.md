## Form rule

Each active character may have two separately managed forms:

1. **Original form** — robot/animal/chibi mascot identity.
2. **Humanized form** — young-adult humanistic interpretation used outside the core mascot presentation.

The two forms are both valid Collectiverse IP and must never overwrite or be mixed with one another in the asset library.

## Variant rule

For any pose with both A and B versions, **B is canonical/approved and A is retained as backup**, unless an explicit pose-specific override is recorded.
