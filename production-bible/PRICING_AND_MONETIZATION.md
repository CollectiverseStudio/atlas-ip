# Collectiverse — Pricing & Monetization Bible

## Revenue Model Overview

Three revenue streams:
1. **Marketplace Commission** (primary) — % on every sale
2. **Subscriptions** (secondary) — monthly/annual plans
3. **Credits** (tertiary) — pay-per-use AI features

## Seller Verification

**Everyone can sell. But first, prove you're real.**

### Why Verification?
- Protects buyers from fraud
- Builds trust in the marketplace
- Required by payment processors anyway
- One-time process (~5 minutes)

### Steps (Porter guides you through):
1. ✅ Verify email
2. 📱 Verify phone (SMS)
3. 🪪 Verify government ID (automated via Stripe)
4. 📦 Confirm shipping address
5. 💳 Link bank account (Stripe Connect)

### What You Get:
- 🛡️ **Verified Seller** badge on all listings
- 🛡️ **Buyer Protection** — all your sales are backed by escrow
- 💰 Direct payouts to your bank account

### FAQ Section

**Q: Can I sell on the Free plan?**
A: Yes! Complete seller verification (5 min one-time) and list unlimited items. You pay 8% only when an item sells.

**Q: What's the difference between Free and Starter?**
A: Starter gives you 5x more AI scans (100/mo), 10x more collection space (1,000 items), and full price data from all sources. Perfect for active collectors.

**Q: Why should I upgrade to Pro?**
A: Two words: save money. Pro reduces your selling commission from 8% to 6%. If you sell $500/month, Pro pays for itself in savings. Plus unlimited items, 500 AI scans, vault access, and multi-channel selling.

**Q: Is there a contract?**
A: No contracts. Cancel anytime. Annual plans save you 2 months — that's the only commitment.

**Q: What happens if I downgrade?**
A: Your data is never deleted. You keep everything — you just can't add beyond the new limit until you're under it. Existing listings stay active.
