# Collectiverse — Pricing & Monetization Bible

> Last updated: 2026-07-06

## Revenue Streams (7 total)

1. **Marketplace Commission** (primary) — % on every sale
2. **Subscriptions** (secondary) — monthly/annual plans (4 tiers)
3. **Credits** (tertiary) — pay-per-use AI features
4. **Promoted Listings** — % of sale for visibility boost
5. **Group Grade Facilitation** — $2-3/card fee for bulk grading coordination
6. **Content Creator Commissions** — platform takes cut of creator referrals
7. **Data API** — Business tier access fee (bundled in subscription)

| Pack | Credits | Price | Per-Credit |
|------|---------|-------|-----------|
| Starter Pack | 25 | $1.99 | $0.08 |
| Value Pack | 100 | $5.99 | $0.06 |
| Power Pack | 500 | $19.99 | $0.04 |

**Monthly included (reset each month):**
- Free: 20
- Starter: 100
- Pro: 500
- Business: Unlimited

**Signup bonus:** 5 credits on first account creation (one-time)

**Our cost per AI call:** $0.01-0.03 → **50%+ margin on all credit packs**

## Seller Verification (required before first sale)

**Everyone on Starter+ can sell. But first, prove you're real.**

### Steps (Porter guides the wizard):
1. ✅ Email verified
2. 📱 Phone verified (SMS)
3. 🪪 Government ID (automated via Stripe Identity)

### What You Get:
- 🛡️ **Verified Seller** badge on all listings
- 🛡️ **Buyer Protection** — all sales backed by escrow
- 💰 Direct payouts to bank account
- Full access to multi-channel selling tools
