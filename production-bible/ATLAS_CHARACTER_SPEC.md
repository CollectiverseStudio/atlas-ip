# Purpose

This document defines the official appearance of Atlas.

Every AI image generator, 3D artist, animator, illustrator, sculptor, and merchandise manufacturer must follow this specification.

Atlas must remain visually consistent across all Collectiverse products.

No deviations are permitted without updating this specification.

# Head

Large rounded helmet. Gloss white finish. Soft reflections. Rounded corners. No sharp edges.

## Helmet
| Attribute | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Material | Gloss painted composite |
| Primary Color | Pure White |
| Trim | Metallic Blue |

## Star
- One only
- Five points
- Perfect symmetry
- Metallic gold
- Mounted on small black cylindrical peg
- Centered perfectly on helmet
- Never oversized
- Never animated independently

## Visor
- Single continuous black visor
- High gloss
- Rounded rectangle
- Soft corners
- No frame visible

## Eyes
- Green illuminated crescents
- Not circles
- Not human
- Not pupils
- Glow softly
- Never overly bright
- Expressions created by: Shape, Angle, Size
- Not by adding eyebrows

## Mouth
- Small orange rounded rectangle
- Very simple
- Minimal animation
- Never large
- Never realistic teeth

## Ear Pieces
- Round silver metallic discs
- One on each side
- Centered vertically
- Flush mounted

---

# Torso

- Rounded armor
- White gloss
- Blue accents
- Friendly proportions

## Chest Badge
- Dark graphite hexagon
- Centered
- Blue capital "C"
- Letter centered precisely
- Badge slightly recessed
- Never omitted

---

# Arms

- Rounded armor
- White gloss
- Blue shoulder accents
- Black segmented elbow joints

## Hands
- Black segmented mechanical fingers
- Five fingers
- Rounded fingertips
- No claws
- Highly expressive

---

# Legs

- Rounded armor
- Compact
- Athletic
- Friendly

## Boots

- Blue trim
- Rounded toe
- Soft sole
- Toy-like
