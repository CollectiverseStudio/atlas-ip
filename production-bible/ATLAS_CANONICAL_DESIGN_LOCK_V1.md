# Atlas Canonical Design Lock v1.0

## Status

**Approved canonical design.**

This document freezes the official Atlas visual identity for Collectiverse. The current chest emblem remains the official mark. The shield-style redesign concepts are rejected and must not replace the current emblem.

---

## Canonical Reference

Atlas is the official Collectiverse mascot, explorer, guide, educator, and brand ambassador.

The approved design is defined by five non-negotiable visual anchors:

1. Gold five-point star antenna.
2. Glossy spherical white head with blue accent panels.
3. Black face visor with neon emerald crescent eyes.
4. Compact white, metallic blue, and piano black robot body.
5. Blue hexagonal white `C` chest emblem.

---

## Chest Emblem Decision

### Approved

The official Atlas chest emblem is the current blue hexagonal badge with a white `C`.

### Rejected

The following proposed alternatives are rejected for canonical use:

- Shield C redesign.
- Connected C redesign.
- Compass C redesign.
- Vault C redesign.
- Core C redesign.
- Any version with hinges, tiny locks, tiny nodes, extra icons, small data marks, or over-detailed internal mechanics.

### Reason

The current emblem is stronger because it is simple, readable, reproducible, and already functions as Atlas's badge. Atlas does not need a complicated chest emblem to explain the full Collectiverse ecosystem. His character, story, and use across the platform carry that meaning.

---

## Official Emblem Rules

The chest emblem must follow these rules:

- Use the official emblem only.
- Do not replace the `C`.
- Do not add shield elements, locks, hinges, nodes, stars, arrows, databases, or extra symbols inside the emblem.
- Do not rotate the emblem.
- Do not stretch the emblem.
- Do not recolor the emblem except through approved lighting and material rendering.
- Do not animate the emblem independently from Atlas unless specifically approved in an animation bible update.
- Always center the emblem on Atlas's torso.
- Target size: approximately 12% of Atlas's total standing height.
- Use metallic blue field, white `C`, dark inner border, and metallic outer bezel.

## Materials

Atlas must be rendered with premium, studio-quality materials:

- White armor: glossy ceramic or polished composite.
- Blue accent panels: anodized metallic blue.
- Black visor, hands, and joints: piano black.
- Eyes: emissive LED glow, neon emerald.
- Star antenna: polished metallic gold.
- Ear discs and mechanical rings: brushed or chrome silver.
- Chest emblem: raised badge with metallic bezel, blue field, white `C`, and visible depth.

---

## Proportions

Atlas should retain these proportions:

- Oversized spherical head: approximately 45% of total character height.
- Compact torso: approximately 25% of total character height.
- Short athletic legs with oversized boots.
- Rounded mechanical hands with expressive fingers.
- Friendly proportions, not intimidating or militarized.

---

## Face and Expression Rules

Atlas's face is a core IP feature.

Allowed:

- Black visor.
- Neon emerald crescent eyes.
- Soft eye glow.
- Small friendly mouth.
- Curious, helpful, smart, playful expressions.

Not allowed:

- Human eyes.
- Eyebrows.
- Blue, red, yellow, or purple eyes.
- Angry villain expression.
- Realistic human mouth.
- Teeth unless specifically approved for a stylized expression sheet.

---

## Star Antenna Rules

The star antenna is a permanent identity anchor.

Rules:

- Always five points.
- Always metallic gold.
- Always mounted at the top of the head.
- Do not replace it with a crown, flag, lightning bolt, antenna ball, flame, planet, or other object.
- Do not remove it from canonical renders.

---

## Pose and Personality Rules

Atlas should read as:

- Helpful.
- Smart.
- Friendly.
- Curious.
- Trustworthy.
- Slightly adventurous.
- A guide for collectors.

Atlas should not read as:

- Aggressive.
- Sarcastic in a mean way.
- Cheap plastic toy unless intentionally producing toy packaging.
- Menacing robot.
- Generic sci-fi android.

## Design Change Control

No future design changes are permitted without:

1. Opening a versioned design-change proposal.
2. Explaining the business reason.
3. Showing before/after visual impact.
4. Assessing trademark, merchandising, animation, and app-icon impact.
5. Explicit approval from Collectiverse Studio.
6. Updating this repository and incrementing the Atlas version.

---

## Current Decision

**Atlas v1.0 remains canonical.**

The existing chest emblem is approved and frozen. Future work should improve rendering quality, vector precision, model consistency, and production asset quality without changing the design.
