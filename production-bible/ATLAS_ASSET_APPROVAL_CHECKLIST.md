# ATLAS ASSET APPROVAL CHECKLIST

## Version

1.0

## Purpose

Use this checklist before publishing, shipping, licensing, uploading, printing, animating, or committing any Atlas asset.

This checklist protects Atlas consistency across images, comics, app UI, social posts, animation, videos, events, and merchandise.

---

## Character Accuracy

- [ ] Atlas matches the canonical Character Specification.
- [ ] Helmet shape is correct.
- [ ] Helmet is white.
- [ ] Metallic blue accents are correct.
- [ ] One gold five-point star is centered on top of the helmet.
- [ ] Star is not oversized.
- [ ] Black visor is correct.
- [ ] Eyes are green crescents.
- [ ] Mouth is small and orange.
- [ ] Ear discs are silver and round.
- [ ] Chest badge / Collectiverse emblem is present when torso is visible.
- [ ] Hands are dark segmented robot hands.
- [ ] Boots are white with blue trim.
- [ ] Body proportions remain friendly and chibi-styled.

---

## Personality Accuracy

- [ ] Atlas appears friendly.
- [ ] Atlas appears curious or helpful.
- [ ] Atlas does not appear mean, threatening, political, offensive, or aggressive.
- [ ] Atlas does not mock collectors.
- [ ] Atlas does not claim something is fake without evidence.
- [ ] Atlas supports the collector as the hero.

---

## Collectible Handling

- [ ] Cards are handled by the edges.
- [ ] Comics are supported carefully.
- [ ] Coins are handled properly.
- [ ] Slabs are held securely.
- [ ] No collectible is bent, thrown, damaged, or treated carelessly.
- [ ] The asset models good collector behavior.

---

## Brand Accuracy

- [ ] Collectiverse association is clear where needed.
- [ ] Tagline usage is correct when present.
- [ ] No third-party logos appear unless approved.
- [ ] No unauthorized brand endorsement is implied.
- [ ] Atlas is not presented as a generic stock robot.

---

## AI / Similarity Review

- [ ] Asset does not resemble another protected character.
- [ ] No third-party franchise universe is implied.
- [ ] No character mashup is present.
- [ ] Prompt did not intentionally request another protected character's identity.
- [ ] Final output is distinguishably Atlas.
- [ ] Prompt and generation notes are retained for important assets.

---

## Technical Quality

- [ ] Image is sharp.
- [ ] Lighting is clean and appropriate.
- [ ] Colors are accurate.
- [ ] No extra fingers, missing limbs, distorted face, or broken geometry.
- [ ] Composition is readable at thumbnail size.
- [ ] Background does not overpower Atlas.
- [ ] File format is appropriate for use.
- [ ] Resolution is sufficient.

---

## Approval Decision

Approve only if all critical items pass.

Reject or revise if:

- Atlas is off-model
- Protected elements are missing
- Similarity risk exists
- Asset conflicts with Brand Bible
- Asset damages trust
- Asset is technically poor
- Asset weakens Atlas recognition
