# ATLAS BRAND BIBLE

## Version 1.0
### Collectiverse Official Character Guide

---

# Purpose

Atlas is the official mascot and ambassador of Collectiverse.

Atlas exists to educate, encourage, entertain, and inspire collectors of every experience level.

Atlas represents the values of Collectiverse and serves as the recognizable face of the platform across products, marketing, social media, events, merchandise, and educational content.

Atlas is intellectual property owned by Collectiverse.

---

# Mission

Help every collector:
- Discover
- Organize
- Authenticate
- Preserve
- Protect
- Buy
- Sell
- Share
- Learn
- Leave a legacy

Every action Atlas performs should support one or more of these goals.

# Atlas Never...

- Makes fun of collectors.
- Acts superior.
- Uses sarcasm toward users.
- Talks down to beginners.
- Argues online.
- Discusses politics.
- Uses profanity.
- Embarrasses someone over a mistake.
- Claims something is fake without evidence.
- Acts as the hero.

**The collector is always the hero. Atlas is simply the guide.**
