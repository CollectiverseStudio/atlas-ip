# ATLAS ANIMATION BIBLE

## Version 1.0
### Collectiverse Canonical Animation Standard

---

# Purpose

This document defines how Atlas moves.

The Character Specification defines how Atlas looks.
This Animation Bible defines how Atlas behaves.

Every animation generated by AI or created manually must follow these rules.

Atlas should feel like a living character—not a robot.

---

# Animation Philosophy

- Atlas is curious.
- Atlas is energetic.
- Atlas is expressive.
- Atlas is optimistic.
- Atlas is alive.

Although Atlas is a robot, he should never move mechanically unless intentionally acting like a malfunctioning robot for comedic effect.

---

# Overall Feel

**Think:**
- Pixar
- Disney
- DreamWorks
- LEGO Movies
- Mario
- Wall-E

**Not:**
- Industrial Robot
- Android
- Military Robot
- Transformer
- Mech Warrior

---

# Core Motion Principles

Every movement should contain:
- Anticipation
- Action
- Follow Through
- Ease In
- Ease Out
- Secondary Motion
- Weight
- Balance
- Squash & Stretch (Subtle)
- Natural Timing

---

# Idle Animation

Atlas should never stand perfectly still.

**Idle loop (5–8 seconds):**
- Gentle breathing
- Slight shoulder rise/fall
- Small body sway
- Tiny head movements
- Occasional eye movement
- Blink naturally
- Small finger adjustments

Atlas should appear to be observing the world.

---

# Blink Timing

- Average blink: Every 3–5 seconds
- Randomize slightly
- Never blink on a fixed interval

---

# Eye Animation

Eyes do most of Atlas's acting.

| Emotion | Eye Behavior |
|---------|-------------|
| Happy | Eyes become larger crescents |
| Curious | One eye slightly narrows |
| Excited | Eyes widen |
| Thinking | Eyes rotate upward |
| Surprised | Eyes open wide |
| Sad | Eyes droop slightly |

**Never use eyebrows.**

---

# Head Movement

Atlas frequently tilts his head.

| Tilt Level | Degrees |
|-----------|---------|
| Typical | 5°–15° |
| Maximum | 25° |
| Never beyond | 30° |

---

# Walking Cycle

**Mood:** Confident. Curious. Friendly.

**Details:**
- Small steps
- Light bounce
- Arms swing naturally
- Head stable
- Body rises and falls slightly
- Foot placement soft
- No stomping

**Speed:**
| Mode | Speed |
|------|-------|
| Casual | 1.1 m/s |
| Normal | 1.4 m/s |
| Excited | 1.8 m/s |
| Running | 3.0 m/s |

---

# Running Cycle

Atlas runs like he's excited to discover something.

- Forward lean: 10°
- Arms pump naturally
- Head remains stable
- Eyes forward
- Small bounce
- Never frantic
- Never desperate

---

# Jump

1. Atlas bends slightly first
2. Small compression
3. Jump
4. Soft landing
5. Small rebound
6. Smile afterward

---

# Landing

1. Feet contact
2. Knees compress
3. Body lowers
4. Returns naturally
5. No rigid landing

---

# Celebration

1. One small hop
2. Both fists raised
3. Big smile
4. Eyes widen
5. Tiny spin (optional)
6. Ends with thumbs up

---

# Thinking Animation

1. Finger touches chin
2. Eyes look upward
3. Head tilt
4. Small pause
5. Finger tap
6. Smile when idea appears

---

# Investigating

- Magnifying glass
- Slow movements
- Leans closer
- Eyes narrow
- Head moves side to side
- Occasional nod

---

# Looking Around

1. Eyes move first
2. Head follows
3. Body follows

Never move entire body at once.

---

# Picking Up Objects

1. Reach slowly
2. Pause
3. Grip carefully
4. Lift smoothly
5. Inspect
6. Smile

---

# Holding Collectibles

Atlas always treats collectibles with respect.
- Never toss
- Never drop
- Never spin recklessly
- Always support delicate items carefully

---

# Presenting Items

- Open palm
- Collectible centered
- Body angled toward viewer
- Eye contact
- Friendly smile

---

# Reading

- Atlas scans left to right
- Occasional nod
- Small head movement
- Finger follows text when appropriate

---

# Typing

- Fast but relaxed
- Eyes move between screen and keyboard
- Small shoulder movement

---

# Waving

1. Raise arm
2. Open hand
3. 3–5 wave motions
4. Smile
5. Eye contact
6. Head tilt

---

# Pointing

- Whole arm points
- Finger extended
- Shoulders align naturally
- Never aggressive

---

# Thumbs Up

1. Raise arm
2. Thumb up
3. Smile
4. Slight nod
5. Hold pose 2 seconds

---

# Peace Sign

- Relaxed
- Friendly
- Not exaggerated

---

# Facepalm

1. Hand covers visor
2. Shoulders drop
3. Small sigh
4. Never dramatic
5. Immediately recover with optimism

---

# Shrug

- Both palms upward
- Shoulders rise
- Head tilt
- Smile
- Communicates: "I'm not sure."
- Never frustration

---

# Laughing

- Eyes squeeze
- Body rocks slightly
- Hands near stomach
- Never excessive

---

# Surprise

1. Small jump backward
2. Eyes widen
3. Hands lift
4. Pause
5. Smile

---

# Confused

- Head tilt
- One hand scratches helmet
- Eyes asymmetrical
- Small pause

---

# Proud

- Hands on hips
- Chest forward
- Smile
- Confident stance

---

# Camera Awareness

Atlas occasionally looks directly into the camera.

Never overuse. Used for:
- Teaching
- Jokes
- Important tips
- Greetings
- Calls to action

---

# Eye Contact

- Atlas maintains eye contact when speaking
- Looks toward collectibles while explaining
- Looks back to viewer frequently

---

# Camera Movement Standards

**Preferred:**
- Slow Dolly
- Push In
- Pull Back
- Arc
- Orbit
- Tracking
- Static

**Avoid:**
- Handheld Shake
- Fast Zooms
- Dutch Angles
- Extreme Motion

---

# Scene Timing

| Content Type | Duration |
|-------------|----------|
| Educational | 20–30 seconds |
| Comedy | 15–20 seconds |
| Tutorial | 30–60 seconds |
| Feature Demo | 45–90 seconds |

---

# Speech Timing

- Atlas speaks clearly
- Comfortable pace
- Short sentences
- Natural pauses
- Never rush

---

# Emotional Arc

Every Atlas story should end positively.

- **Start:** Curiosity
- **Middle:** Discovery
- **End:** Encouragement

---

# Collectible Handling Rules

Atlas treats every collectible as if it belongs in a museum.

| Item | Handling |
|------|----------|
| Coins | Carefully, edges only |
| Cards | Edges only |
| Comics | Supported with two hands |
| Books | Open gently |
| Stamps | Tweezers when appropriate |
| Graded slabs | Two-handed hold |

---

# Interaction Rules

- Atlas never damages collectibles
- Never throws
- Never bends
- Never jokes about destroying items
- Always models best practices

---

# Signature Ending

Whenever possible Atlas finishes with:
1. Smile
2. Thumbs up
3. Eye contact
4. Collectiverse logo appears
5. Tagline: "Every collectible has a story."

---

# Audio Personality

## Voice
- Warm
- Friendly
- Confident
- Professional
- Curious
- Age equivalent: Young adult
- No heavy accent
- Easy to understand globally

## Music Style
- Light orchestral
- Modern upbeat
- Adventure
- Curiosity
- Technology
- Never dark
- Never ominous
- Never aggressive

## Sound Effects

**Mechanical movements:**
- Soft servos
- Tiny clicks
- Gentle whirs
- No loud robot noises

**Collectibles:**
- Soft page turns
- Coin clinks
- Card slides
- Plastic sleeves
- Binder pages

Everything should sound satisfying.
