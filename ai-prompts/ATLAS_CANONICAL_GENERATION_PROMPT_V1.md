# Atlas Canonical Generation Prompt v1.0

Use this prompt whenever generating Atlas images, comics, animation frames, model sheets, social assets, or merchandise mockups.

---

## Canonical Prompt

Atlas is the official mascot of Collectiverse: a friendly premium robot explorer with a glossy spherical white head, black face visor, neon emerald crescent eyes, small happy orange mouth, polished white armor, metallic Collectiverse-blue accent panels, piano-black robotic hands and joints, oversized rounded boots, silver ear-disc side modules, and a polished gold five-point star antenna mounted on top of his head. Atlas has a compact athletic body, oversized friendly head, expressive mechanical hands, and a helpful intelligent personality.

Atlas wears the official blue hexagonal Collectiverse chest emblem: a metallic blue hexagon badge with a clean white capital `C`, dark inner border, and raised metallic outer bezel. The emblem must remain simple and unchanged. Do not redesign it. Do not add shield details, locks, hinges, nodes, stars, arrows, databases, or extra symbols to the chest emblem.

Render Atlas as a studio-quality 3D character with premium materials, clean reflections, soft lighting, crisp edges, polished surfaces, and a friendly collectible-platform mascot feel. Atlas should look trustworthy, smart, curious, helpful, and brand-safe.

---

## Required Visual Anchors

- Gold five-point star antenna.
- Spherical glossy white head.
- Blue head accent panels.
- Black face visor.
- Neon emerald crescent eyes.
- Small friendly mouth with orange interior.
- White armor.
- Metallic blue accents.
- Piano-black joints and hands.
- Silver circular ear modules.
- Official blue hexagonal white `C` chest emblem.
- Compact body with oversized boots.

---

## Negative Prompt / Do Not Generate

Do not generate Atlas with:

- A changed chest emblem.
- A shield-style emblem replacing the current badge.
- Extra symbols inside the chest emblem.
- Different eye color.
- Human eyes.
- Eyebrows.
- Missing star antenna.
- A crown instead of the star.
- A flattened head.
- A tall humanoid body.
- Weapons.
- Angry or villain styling.
- Rusted, damaged, horror, military, or dystopian styling.
- Low-quality plastic toy finish unless specifically requested for a toy mockup.
- Incorrect letters on the chest.
- Text labels floating on the character unless specifically requested for a poster or infographic.

## Asset Output Preferences

Use transparent background for reusable assets unless a scene or poster layout is requested.

For production-grade exports, create:

- PNG with transparency.
- WebP optimized app asset.
- SVG for emblem and wordmark assets where applicable.
- 4K master render for hero artwork.
- Square 1024x1024 app/social variant when needed.
