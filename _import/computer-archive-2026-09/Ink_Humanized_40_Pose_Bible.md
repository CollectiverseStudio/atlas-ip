# Ink — Humanized Character Production Bible & 40-Pose Catalog

**Status:** LOCKED / 40 OF 40 VERIFIED

## Character Lock

- **Faction:** Keeper (Hero)
- **Role:** Historian / Storyteller
- **Humanized identity:** Indian young woman
- **Complexion:** Warm brown
- **Hair:** Long black hair; preserve the approved full, flowing silhouette
- **Eyes:** Deep brown
- **Build:** Slender / graceful young-adult proportions
- **Wardrobe:** Indigo/navy historian ensemble with purple layering, restrained gold detailing, feather-inspired cape panels, dark fitted trousers, black/gold boots, Keeper C badge
- **Signature prop:** Purple-and-gold quill; books, scrolls and archival materials when pose-appropriate
- **Personality:** Thoughtful, wise, patient, poetic, empathetic; capable of playful confidence

### Continuity Rules

Keep the same face, apparent age, complexion, hair, body proportions, wardrobe construction, emblem, footwear and core palette. Human anatomy only. Feather motifs are clothing/cape elements, not literal wings. No pose number, title, caption, watermark or QA text is to be embedded in the individual pose artwork.
