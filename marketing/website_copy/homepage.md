### Module 5: Passport

#### Headline
**CarFax for Collectibles**

#### Body
Every item deserves a story you can prove. Digital Passports create a permanent, verifiable record of ownership, authentication, and condition history that travels with your collectible forever — building trust and protecting value.

#### Key Features
- **Ownership history** — Complete chain of custody, owner by owner
- **Authentication records** — Link PSA, BGS, CGC, and other certifications
- **Verification badges** — Items with Passport earn trust indicators
- **Grading history** — Track grades and condition changes over time
- **Transfer on sale** — Passport follows the item to the new owner automatically
- **Insurance documentation** — Instant proof of ownership and value
- **QR verification** — Anyone can scan and verify authenticity
- **Value uplift** — Passport items typically sell for 15–25% more
