## FAQ Section

### Can I switch plans anytime?
Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time. When you upgrade, you get immediate access to new features. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period. Pro-rated adjustments apply.

### Do I need Pro to sell?
No — selling is an add-on available on any plan (including Free). You just need Seller Verification and the Selling add-on activated.

### What payment methods do you accept?
Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal for subscriptions and credit purchases.

### What happens if I cancel?
Your collection data stays safe. You retain Free tier access forever. Blockchain certificates remain on-chain permanently. You can re-subscribe anytime to restore premium features.

### Is there a contract or commitment?
No contracts. Monthly plans can be cancelled anytime. Annual plans are paid upfront with a 17% discount — no early cancellation penalty, but no pro-rated refunds on annual billing.

### Can I try Pro before committing?
Start with the Free plan and buy a Starter credit pack ($5 for 25 credits) to try premium features. When you're ready for unlimited, upgrade to Pro.
