## Getting Started

### 1. What is Collectiverse?

Collectiverse is the complete platform for collectors — a single place to organize, protect, buy, sell, and preserve your collections across 22+ categories. Whether you collect sports cards, Pokémon, coins, comics, memorabilia, toys, or anything else, Collectiverse brings inventory management, a marketplace, live selling, event discovery, digital passports, AI identification, and estate planning together in one place.

### 2. How do I create an account?

Visit collectiverse.studio and click "Start Your Collection." You can sign up with email or use Google/Apple sign-in. Choose between an Individual (personal) or Business account type. The Free tier gives you immediate access — no credit card required.

### 3. Is Collectiverse really free?

Yes. The Free tier lets you catalog up to 500 items, browse and buy on the marketplace, use the Store Finder, discover events, and access community features at no cost. Premium features like AI appraisals, professional photo editing, and blockchain certificate minting require credits, available through Pro ($9.99/month) or Business ($29.99/month) subscriptions, or purchasable credit packs.

### 4. What platforms is Collectiverse available on?

- **Web app:** collectiverse.studio (any browser)
- **Progressive Web App (PWA):** Install from your browser for a native-like experience
- **iOS:** App Store
- **Android:** Google Play
- **Collectiverse Events:** events.collectiverse.studio (dedicated events companion app)
- **Collectiverse Live:** live.collectiverse.studio (dedicated live streaming companion app)

### 5. What collecting categories does Collectiverse support?

We support 22+ categories including: Sports Cards, Pokémon, Magic: The Gathering, Yu-Gi-Oh!, Comic Books, Coins, Currency, Autographs, Memorabilia, Action Figures, Funko Pops, Video Games, Vinyl Records, Books, Movie Memorabilia, Vintage Toys, Die-Cast Cars, Stamps, Watches, Sneakers, Art Prints, and Tickets/Events. Plus custom categories for anything else.

### 6. Can I use Collectiverse if I only collect one thing?

Absolutely. Many users focus on a single category. Collectiverse gives you category-specific tools and fields tailored to what you collect. But when you inevitably pick up a second hobby (it happens to the best of us), everything's already in one place.

### 7. What's the difference between Individual and Business accounts?

Individual accounts are for personal collectors. Business accounts are for shops, dealers, and resellers — they include business name display, business tax documentation, and multi-user access (coming soon). Both account types can access any plan tier and any add-on.

## Inventory

### 8. How do I add items to my collection?

Three ways:
1. **Smart Capture:** Take a photo — Atlas AI identifies the item and fills all fields automatically
2. **Catalog Search:** Search our 5.75M+ reference catalog and add with one tap
3. **Manual Entry:** Create custom entries for items not in our catalog

### 9. What is Smart Capture and how accurate is it?

Smart Capture uses Atlas AI's computer vision to identify collectibles from a single photograph. Point your camera at any supported item — Atlas identifies it, matches against our catalog, pre-fills all fields, removes the background, and provides a market value estimate. Accuracy is highest for well-documented items (major card sets, popular comics, common coins). For rare or obscure items, Atlas provides a confidence score and may suggest manual verification.

### 10. Can I import my collection from other platforms?

Yes. We support CSV and spreadsheet imports from most popular collection management tools. Our import wizard maps your existing data fields to Collectiverse fields, and Atlas AI helps fill gaps. We have platform-specific import guides for common tools.

### 11. Is there a limit on how many items I can add?

Free plan: 500 items. Pro and Business plans: unlimited. There's no practical upper limit on paid plans — we have users with 100,000+ items.

### 12. Can I organize items into folders and sub-collections?

Yes. Create unlimited folders, sub-collections, and custom groupings. Organize by category, set, year, grade, value, location, or any criteria you choose. Nested folders are supported.

### 13. Can I track where my items are physically stored?

Yes. Location tracking lets you tag items by physical location — display case, safe, storage unit, binder page, grading submission, on loan, or any custom location. Always know exactly where everything lives.

### 14. How does collection valuation work?

We aggregate real-time sales data from eBay sold listings, major auction results, and our own marketplace to calculate fair market values. Values update regularly, and you can view trends over time for individual items and your entire portfolio.

## Marketplace

### 15. How does buying and selling work?

Sellers list items with photos, descriptions, and pricing (fixed price, best offer, or auction). Buyers browse, search, and purchase directly. Payments are processed securely through Stripe. Passport Trust records transfer automatically at sale. The Selling add-on is required to list items.

### 16. What are the selling fees?

The Selling add-on has no monthly fee — just 5% per successful sale. Business tier members enjoy reduced marketplace fees. No listing fees for any plan.

### 17. Can I list items on Collectiverse and eBay simultaneously?

Yes. eBay integration lets you sync inventory and manage cross-listings from one dashboard. List once, sell everywhere — no double-selling risk because inventory syncs across channels.

### 18. What payment methods can buyers use?

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and PayPal. All payments processed securely through Stripe (PCI-DSS compliant).

### 19. How does dispute resolution work?

If a buyer and seller disagree, either party can open a dispute. Our process includes: (1) Direct communication period, (2) Evidence review including Passport Trust records, photos, and tracking info, (3) Collectiverse mediation and determination. Passport Trust records serve as powerful evidence in disputes.

### 20. Do I need a paid plan to sell?

No. The Selling add-on is available on any plan, including Free. You just need to complete Seller Verification and activate the add-on.

## Live

### 21. What can I do with Collectiverse Live?

Host live streams with real-time selling, including: live breaks (randomized spot sales with live reveals), live auctions (timed bidding), flash sales, and showcase streams. Viewers can purchase items directly during your stream without leaving the video.

### 22. Do I need special equipment to stream?

No. You can stream from your phone's camera, a webcam, or professional equipment. The Live interface works on mobile and desktop. Good lighting and a stable internet connection are all you really need to get started.

### 23. What's the cost to go live?

The Live Hosting add-on is $14.99/month. This gives you access to all live selling features including breaks, auctions, in-stream purchases, and revenue management. Pro plan (or higher) is recommended for the best streaming experience.

### 24. Can viewers buy items during my stream?

Yes — that's the core feature. You feature items during your stream, and viewers can purchase with a single tap without leaving the video. In-stream purchases are tracked, and items are automatically marked as sold in your inventory.

## Passport & Authentication

### 29. What is a Digital Passport?

A Digital Passport is a permanent, verifiable record attached to a collectible item. It tracks ownership history (chain of custody), authentication records, grading history, condition changes, and sale history. Think of it as CarFax for collectibles — building trust and protecting value over time.

### 30. How does blockchain authentication work?

When you mint a certificate of authenticity, we create an NFT on the Solana blockchain using the Metaplex protocol. This creates a permanent, publicly verifiable record that cannot be altered or deleted. You don't need a crypto wallet — we handle the blockchain infrastructure. If you have a Solana wallet, you can optionally view certificates there too.

### 31. Is Passport required for all items?

No. You can catalog items without Passport records. However, items with complete Passport provenance earn trust badges, rank higher in marketplace search, and typically sell for 15–25% more because buyers trust verified provenance.

### 32. What happens to Passports when I sell an item?

The Passport transfers automatically to the buyer, adding the new ownership record to the chain. The buyer inherits the full provenance history, and you're recorded as a verified previous owner. This is the entire point — provenance that follows the item for life.

### 33. Can Atlas detect counterfeits?

Yes. Atlas analyzes visual characteristics including printing patterns, color accuracy, card stock texture, holographic elements, and known counterfeit markers. When potential authenticity concerns are detected, Atlas flags the item with specific reasons. This is a supplementary tool — for high-value items, we always recommend professional third-party authentication.

### 34. Does minting a blockchain certificate prove my item is authentic?

No — blockchain minting proves that a certificate was created at a specific time and cannot be altered. It does not independently verify the physical item's authenticity. The strength comes from combining blockchain records with authentication documentation, provenance history, and community verification to build comprehensive trust.

## Credits & Pricing

### 35. What are credits?

Credits are Collectiverse's universal currency for premium features. They're used for photo editing, background removal, AI grading appraisals, and blockchain minting. Pro plans include 30 credits/month, Business includes 100/month, and additional packs can be purchased anytime.

### 36. How much do credits cost?

Plan inclusions: Free = 0/month, Pro = 30/month, Business = 100/month. Credit packs: Starter (25 credits, $5), Standard (100 credits, $15), Pro (500 credits, $60), Bulk (1,500 credits, $150). The more you buy, the less per credit.

### 37. Do credits expire?

**Purchased credits never expire.** Monthly subscription credits reset each billing cycle — unused monthly credits do not roll over to the next month. If you buy credit packs, those remain in your account indefinitely.

### 38. What's the difference between Free, Pro, and Business?

- **Free ($0/month):** 500 items, 1 collection, 1 GB storage, browse/buy, basic features
- **Pro ($9.99/month):** Unlimited items, 30 credits/month, import/export, analytics, API, Smart Capture, full Atlas AI, 25 GB storage
- **Business ($29.99/month):** Everything in Pro + 100 credits/month, store profile, bulk tools, tax tools, multi-user (coming soon), 100 GB storage, priority support

### 39. Can I switch plans?

Yes, anytime. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period. Pro-rated adjustments apply for mid-cycle changes.

### 40. Is there an annual discount?

Yes. Both Pro and Business offer 17% savings when billed annually ($99/year for Pro, $299/year for Business).

### 41. What happens if I downgrade or cancel?

Your collection data stays safe. You retain Free tier access forever — your items, photos, and records don't disappear. You lose access to premium features (credits, advanced AI, bulk tools, etc.). Blockchain certificates remain on-chain permanently. Re-subscribe anytime to restore premium access.

### 42. Can I get a refund on credits?

Purchased credits are non-refundable once added to your account. If a credit-powered action fails due to a system error (not user error), the credits are automatically refunded to your balance.

## Estate Planning

### 43. What is estate planning on Collectiverse?

Estate Planning ensures your collections are protected and properly handled in the event of your passing. You designate beneficiaries, set liquidation preferences, write legacy letters about meaningful items, and assign trusted contacts who can initiate the transfer process.

### 44. Does estate planning cost anything?

Basic setup is free — designate beneficiaries, set preferences, write instructions at no cost. Professional valuations ($99–$499 depending on collection size) and managed liquidation services (5% fee on successful sales) are available as optional paid services.

### 45. How does the transfer process work?

When a trusted contact initiates an estate claim: (1) Death verification is required with official documentation, (2) Collectiverse reviews and verifies the claim, (3) Collection access transfers to designated beneficiaries, (4) Passport records update to reflect inheritance, (5) Beneficiaries receive full access with guidance. The process is secure, respectful, and legally compliant.

### 46. Do my beneficiaries need Collectiverse accounts?

No. Beneficiaries are guided through account creation during the transfer process if they don't already have accounts. They don't need to do anything in advance.

### 47. Can I assign different items to different people?

Yes. You can assign your entire collection to one person, or designate specific items, sub-collections, or categories to different beneficiaries. Each item can have its own handling instructions (keep, sell, or donate).

## Security & Privacy

### 48. Is my data secure?

Yes. We employ industry-standard security practices:
- **Encryption:** All data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
- **Authentication:** Multi-factor authentication available on all accounts
- **Infrastructure:** Hosted on AWS with enterprise-grade security
- **Payments:** PCI-DSS compliant processing through Stripe (we never store card numbers)
- **Privacy:** GDPR, CCPA, and COPPA compliant

### 49. Do you store my ID or personal documents?

No. Identity verification is handled by Stripe/Persona — we only receive a pass/fail result. We never store, view, or have access to your identity documents.

### 50. What happens to my data if I delete my account?

We remove your personal data per our retention policy. Collection catalog data is deleted. However, blockchain records remain on-chain (they are immutable by nature) and Passport records that were transferred to other users remain in their accounts.

### 51. Is there an API?

Yes. API access is available for Pro and Business tier subscribers. Business tier gets advanced API access with higher rate limits and additional endpoints. Documentation is available at docs.collectiverse.studio.

### 52. What's the minimum age to use Collectiverse?

Users must be 13 years or older (COPPA compliance). Users under 18 may need parental consent for marketplace transactions and blockchain minting. See our Terms of Service for full age-related policies.

### 53. Who can see my collection?

You control visibility. Collections are private by default. You can choose to make items or collections public (visible to the community), share with specific users, or keep everything completely private. Only you can see your full inventory unless you explicitly share it.

### 54. How do you handle my photos?

Photos are stored encrypted on AWS infrastructure. We use them to display your items within the platform and for AI features you explicitly invoke (Smart Capture, appraisals). We don't sell, share, or use your photos for training purposes without consent.
