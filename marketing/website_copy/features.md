## Module 1: Inventory

### Section Headline
📦 **Inventory — Know Exactly What You Have**

### Section Subtitle
The most comprehensive collection management system ever built for collectors. Smart, fast, and designed for how you actually organize your stuff.

### Features

#### Unlimited Items
No arbitrary caps. Pro and Business plans support unlimited items across unlimited collections. Whether you have 10 cards or 100,000, Collectiverse scales with you.

#### Location Tracking
Tag items by physical location — display case, safe, storage unit, on loan, or in transit. Always know where everything lives.

#### Purchase History
Record what you paid, where you bought it, and when. Track ROI across your entire portfolio and see which purchases have appreciated most.

#### High-Resolution Photos
Upload multiple photos per item — front, back, close-ups of condition issues, and more. Photos are stored in full resolution with zoom capability.

#### Folders & Sub-Collections
Organize your way. Create nested folders, themed sub-collections, or custom groupings. Build a PSA set registry, organize by sport, or sort by decade — your choice.

#### Bulk Import
Migrating from another platform? Import via CSV or spreadsheet. Our import wizard maps your existing data fields and Atlas AI fills any gaps automatically.

#### Custom Fields
Every category has specialized attributes built in (PSA grade, mint mark, print run, pressing info, etc.), plus you can create unlimited custom fields for anything else.

#### Condition Tracking
Log condition at acquisition and track changes over time with photo evidence. Essential for insurance claims and resale documentation.

#### Category-Specific Schemas
Each of our 22+ categories has purpose-built data fields. A sports card entry looks different from a coin entry, which looks different from a vinyl record. Because they should.

#### Collection Analytics
See your collection's total value, value trends over time, category breakdowns, and top performers — all updated with real market data.

## Module 5: Passport

### Section Headline
🛡️ **Passport — CarFax for Collectibles**

### Section Subtitle
Create permanent, verifiable records of ownership, authenticity, and condition history. Build trust. Protect value. Prove provenance.

### Features

#### Ownership History
Every Passport tracks the complete chain of custody — who owned it, when they got it, and when it changed hands. Immutable and verifiable.

#### Authentication Records
Link existing certifications from PSA, BGS, CGC, CBCS, and other grading services. Or add self-authentication for items you've personally verified.

#### Verification Badges
Items with complete Passport records earn trust indicators in the marketplace. Buyers see at a glance that provenance is verified.

#### Grading History
Track grade assignments and condition changes over time with photo evidence. See how an item has been treated across its lifetime.

#### Transfer on Sale
When you sell an item through Collectiverse, the Passport transfers automatically to the buyer. They inherit the full history, and you're recorded as a verified previous owner.

#### Insurance Documentation
Instant proof of ownership and value for insurance claims. Export reports in formats insurance companies accept.

#### QR Verification
Each Passport has a scannable QR code. Anyone — buyers, grading companies, auction houses — can verify authenticity instantly.

#### Community Verification
High-trust community members can add verification observations. Multiple verifications build stronger trust scores.

#### Transfer with Sale
When items sell, blockchain certificates transfer to the new owner's Collectiverse account automatically.

### Section Subtitle
Turn a single photo into a fully cataloged item in seconds. No manual data entry. No searching. Just point, shoot, and let Atlas handle the rest.

### How It Works

1. **📷 Snap a Photo** — Take a picture of your collectible (card, coin, comic, toy — anything)
2. **🔍 Atlas Identifies** — AI recognizes the item and matches it against our catalog
3. **✨ Background Removed** — Clean, professional image generated automatically
4. **📋 Fields Populated** — Title, year, set, brand, manufacturer, condition estimate, and market value pre-filled
5. **✅ Saved to Collection** — One tap to confirm and add to your inventory

### Key Details
- Works across all 22+ categories
- Matches against 5.75M+ reference records
- Pre-fills category-specific fields automatically
- Background removal creates professional presentation photos
- Works offline — sync when connected
- Confidence scores show identification certainty
- Manual override available if Atlas needs correction
