## Strategy Overview

**Goal:** Build a network of 50+ authentic influencer advocates across collecting categories who genuinely use and recommend Collectiverse to their audience.

**Philosophy:** We don't want ads — we want believers. Target influencers who are real collectors, will actually use the product, and whose audience trusts their recommendations.

**Budget:** $30,000 over 90 days (scaling from product-only to paid partnerships)

## Content Briefs for Influencers

### Brief 1: "First Impressions" Video

**Goal:** Authentic first look at the platform
**Duration:** 10-15 minutes
**Key Moments to Capture:**
- Opening the app for the first time
- Setting up their profile and interests
- Using Atlas AI to identify their first item (genuine reaction!)
- Browsing the reference database
- Exploring the Store Finder near their location
- Overall thoughts and who it's for

**Talking Points (suggestions, not script):**
- "This identifies [item type] from a photo"
- "It knows 5.75 million collectibles"
- "Digital Passports are like CarFax for your collection"
- "It's free to start — Pro unlocks [features]"
- Use affiliate link/code in description

### Brief 2: "Organize My Collection" Video

**Goal:** Show real-world use case of bulk cataloging
**Duration:** 15-25 minutes
**Key Moments:**
- Show the "before" (boxes, binders, chaos)
- Use Smart Capture / Atlas for bulk scanning
- Show the organized collection dashboard after
- Highlight value discovery ("I didn't know this was worth $X!")
- Time comparison (old method vs Collectiverse)

### Brief 3: "Is This Worth Money?" TikTok/Reel

**Goal:** Viral short-form content showing Atlas AI
**Duration:** 30-60 seconds
**Format:**
- Hook: "I found this [item] at a thrift store..."
- Show item to camera
- Open Atlas, take photo, get identification + value
- Reaction: surprise at value (or "well, that's disappointing" — either works!)
- CTA: "Link in bio to try yourself"

## Legal & Compliance

### FTC Disclosure Requirements

All influencers MUST:
- Include #ad or #sponsored on paid content
- Verbally disclose "This video is sponsored by Collectiverse" within first 30 seconds
- Include written disclosure in video description
- Not make unsubstantiated claims about earnings/value

### Contract Essentials

Every paid partnership includes:
- Content approval window (24 hours, single round of feedback)
- Usage rights (we can repost/repurpose with credit)
- Exclusivity clause (no competitor promotion for 30 days)
- Performance minimums (must hit promised delivery date)
- FTC compliance responsibility
- Early termination clause (both parties, 7-day notice)
- Payment terms (50% upfront, 50% on publication)

### Brand Safety Guidelines

**DO:**
- Be authentic and share genuine opinions
- Show real use of the product
- Mention limitations/areas for improvement (builds trust)
- Use their own creative style
- Engage with comments on sponsored content

**DON'T:**
- Make investment/financial guarantees
- Claim items are worth specific amounts (say "estimated")
- Bash competitor products by name (differentiate, don't attack)
- Use children to promote (COPPA compliance)
- Share user data or collection details without consent
