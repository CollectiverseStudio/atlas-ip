## OUTREACH BEST PRACTICES

### Before Sending:
- [ ] Research the creator's content — reference at least ONE specific video/post/episode
- [ ] Personalize the subject line with their name or channel name
- [ ] Confirm their contact email (check channel About/bio, not random guess)
- [ ] Verify they cover categories Collectiverse supports
- [ ] Check for existing brand deals that might conflict

### Follow-Up Cadence:
- **Day 0:** Initial email (above templates)
- **Day 4:** Brief follow-up ("Bumping this up — happy to just send a Pro account link if easier")
- **Day 10:** Final follow-up ("Last note from me — offer stands whenever you're ready")
- **No response after 3 touches:** Move to nurture list (engage with their content organically)

### Tracking:
- Use unique affiliate links per creator for attribution
- Track: response rate, activation rate (actually used the account), content created, signups driven
- Goal: 20% response rate, 10% activation, 5% create content

### Legal Notes:
- All sponsored/affiliate content must comply with FTC disclosure requirements
- Creators must disclose partnership/free product in content
- Include this requirement in partnership agreements
