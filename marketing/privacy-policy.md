## 5. Specific Data Practices

### 5.1 Collection Data
Your collection data (items, values, organization, notes) is yours. We do not sell your collection data to third parties. Aggregated, anonymized collection data (e.g., "30% of users collect trading cards") may be used for market research and platform improvement, but individual collections are never shared without your consent.

### 5.2 Photos and Images
- Photos you upload remain your property (see our Terms of Service for license grants)
- Smart Capture images may be retained to improve AI model accuracy, but are stripped of identifying user information before use in training
- You can delete your photos at any time through the Platform
- Live stream recordings may be retained per our content retention policy

### 5.3 Blockchain Data
**Important:** Data written to the Solana blockchain is **public and permanent by design.** When you mint a blockchain certificate of authenticity:
- Item metadata (description, authentication data) is recorded on-chain
- The associated wallet address is publicly visible
- This data **cannot be deleted, modified, or made private** after minting
- Anyone can view blockchain records using public Solana explorers

We do not publicly link blockchain wallet addresses to your Collectiverse identity unless you choose to make that connection public. However, the on-chain data itself is permanent and outside our ability to remove.

### 5.4 Estate Planning Data
- Beneficiary designations, trusted contacts, and estate instructions are encrypted at rest with additional access controls
- This data is only accessed when an estate claim is initiated through our verification process
- We do not share estate planning data with any third party except as required by valid legal process
- You can modify or delete estate planning designations at any time through the Platform

## 9. Your Privacy Rights

### 9.1 All Users
Regardless of location, you have the right to:
- **Access:** Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
- **Correction:** Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
- **Deletion:** Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal retention requirements and blockchain immutability)
- **Data Export:** Download your collection data and personal information in a machine-readable format
- **Marketing Opt-Out:** Unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time
- **Consent Withdrawal:** Withdraw consent for optional data processing

### 9.2 European Economic Area (EEA) / UK — GDPR Rights
If you are in the EEA or UK, you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation:
- **Right to be Forgotten:** Request erasure of your data (with exceptions for legal obligations and blockchain data)
- **Right to Restriction:** Request we restrict processing of your data
- **Right to Object:** Object to processing based on legitimate interests
- **Right to Portability:** Receive your data in a structured, commonly used format
- **Automated Decision-Making:** Right not to be subject to decisions based solely on automated processing (AI valuations are advisory, not determinative)
- **Lodge Complaint:** File a complaint with your local data protection authority

**Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR):**
- Contract performance (account, transactions, services)
- Legitimate interest (security, fraud prevention, analytics, product improvement)
- Consent (marketing, optional AI training, location services)
- Legal obligation (tax records, regulatory compliance)

**Data Transfer:** Your data may be transferred to and processed in the United States. We use Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and appropriate supplementary measures to ensure adequate protection.

### 9.3 California Residents — CCPA/CPRA Rights
If you are a California resident, you have rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act / California Privacy Rights Act:

- **Right to Know:** Request disclosure of categories and specific pieces of personal information collected
- **Right to Delete:** Request deletion of personal information (subject to exceptions)
- **Right to Correct:** Request correction of inaccurate information
- **Right to Opt-Out of Sale/Sharing:** We do **not** sell your personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
- **Right to Limit Use of Sensitive Personal Information:** Request we limit use of sensitive information to what is necessary for services
- **Right to Non-Discrimination:** We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights

**Categories of Information Collected (per CCPA):**
- Identifiers (name, email, IP address)
- Commercial information (purchase history, collection data)
- Internet activity (usage data, browsing history on Platform)
- Geolocation data (with consent)
- Audio/visual information (photos, live streams)
- Professional information (for Business tier store profiles)
- Inferences (AI-derived item identifications, value estimates)

**Shine the Light:** California residents may request information about disclosure of personal information to third parties for direct marketing. Contact us at privacy@collectiverse.studio.

### 9.4 Other Jurisdictions
We comply with applicable data protection laws in all jurisdictions where we operate, including but not limited to: Canada (PIPEDA), Australia (Privacy Act 1988), Brazil (LGPD), and Japan (APPI). Contact us for jurisdiction-specific information.

## 10. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your information:

- **Encryption in Transit:** TLS 1.3 for all data transmission
- **Encryption at Rest:** AES-256 encryption for stored data
- **Access Controls:** Role-based access, principle of least privilege
- **Multi-Factor Authentication:** Available on all accounts, required for internal access
- **Infrastructure:** Hosted on AWS with SOC 2, ISO 27001 certified infrastructure
- **Payment Security:** PCI-DSS compliant processing through Stripe
- **Regular Audits:** Periodic security assessments and penetration testing
- **Incident Response:** Documented breach notification procedures (72 hours for GDPR)
- **Employee Training:** Regular privacy and security training for all team members

**No system is 100% secure.** While we implement industry-standard protections, we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it to security@collectiverse.studio.
