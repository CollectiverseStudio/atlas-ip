## What is Collectiverse?

**Collectiverse is the system of record for collectibles.** It's a single platform where you can catalog, authenticate, value, buy, sell, discover, and protect your collection — regardless of what you collect.

Think of it this way:
- **For your items:** It's a digital passport with verified provenance (like CarFax for collectibles)
- **For identification:** It's an AI expert that knows 5.75 million items across 14 categories
- **For buying/selling:** It's a live marketplace with streaming, breaks, and built-in trust
- **For your legacy:** It's estate planning that ensures your collection outlives you

## The Passport Trust System

This is the heart of Collectiverse.

Every item you add gets a **Passport** — a living record of provenance that travels with the item through every transaction. It captures:

- **Authentication records** — grading certs, expert opinions, AI analysis
- **Ownership history** — a verified chain of who owned it and when
- **Condition tracking** — photos and notes at every stage
- **Transaction history** — what it sold for, where, and to whom
- **Blockchain verification** — optional NFT certificates on Solana for immutable proof

When you buy something with a Collectiverse Passport, you know its history. When you sell, you offer trust that no other platform provides. It's a competitive advantage for honest sellers and peace of mind for careful buyers.

## Meet Atlas: Your AI Collecting Assistant

Atlas is our AI assistant, trained specifically for collectibles identification and valuation.

**Smart Capture:** Point your phone at any collectible. Atlas identifies the item, fills in all catalog fields (year, set, variant, manufacturer, condition estimate), provides a current market value range, and flags potential counterfeits.

**What Atlas does:**
- Identifies items across all 14 categories from photos
- Estimates current fair market value from real sales data
- Detects counterfeits by analyzing visual markers and known fake patterns
- Provides buy/hold/sell recommendations based on market trends
- Auto-catalogs items without manual data entry

Atlas is built into every part of the platform. It's like having an expert dealer for every category sitting next to you.
