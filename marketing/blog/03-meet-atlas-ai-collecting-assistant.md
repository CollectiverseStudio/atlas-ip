## Smart Capture: One Photo, Full Catalog Entry

This is the feature people can't stop talking about.

### How It Works:

1. **Open Smart Capture** in the Collectiverse app
2. **Point your camera** at any collectible
3. **Atlas analyzes the image** in seconds
4. **Results appear:**
   - Item identification (name, set, year, variant, edition)

## Counterfeit Detection: Trust, But Verify

This is where Atlas gets serious.

Counterfeiting is the single biggest trust problem in collecting. Fake graded cards, counterfeit coins, forged signatures, reproduction comics with fake CGC labels — the problem is massive and growing.

### How Atlas Detects Counterfeits:

**Visual Analysis**
- Compares print quality, color saturation, centering against known authentic examples
- Analyzes card stock texture and edge patterns
- Checks holographic elements against reference images

**Label Verification**
- Analyzes grading company labels (PSA, CGC, PCGS, etc.) for font inconsistencies, alignment issues, and known counterfeit patterns
- Cross-references cert numbers when visible

**Pattern Matching**
- Trained on databases of known counterfeits and their telltale signs
- Flags items that match known fake signatures (trimming evidence, recoloring, artificial aging)

**Confidence Scoring**
- Each item gets an authenticity confidence score
- Items below threshold get flagged with specific concerns
- Higher-risk items are recommended for physical authentication

### What You See:
```
⚠️ COUNTERFEIT ALERT — Confidence: LOW (38%)
Concerns flagged:
  • Label font inconsistent with PSA Gen 9 labels
  • Case seam pattern doesn't match reference
  • Cert number not found in PSA database
Recommendation: Do NOT purchase without physical verification
```

Atlas won't replace professional authentication for high-value items — but it can save you from obvious fakes and alert you when something deserves closer scrutiny.
