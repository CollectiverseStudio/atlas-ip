## Partner With Collectiverse to Power Your Events

Collectiverse is the collecting platform with a growing base of active collectors across **14 categories**. Our Events Discovery system puts your show, convention, or meetup directly in front of collectors searching for events near them.

We provide the technology. You provide the venue. Collectors get an amazing experience.

## 🤝 Premium Partnership Benefits

For recurring shows and larger events, we offer enhanced partnership:

### 📣 Promoted Placement
Featured position in our events feed and targeted push notifications to collectors in your area who match your event's categories.

### 🤖 On-Site Atlas AI Stations
We provide Atlas AI demo stations at your event. Attendees bring items, scan them, get instant identification and values. **Massive engagement driver** — collectors love it.

### 💰 Live Breaks Coordination
We can coordinate or support live breaks during your event — managed selling with built-in payments and shipping. Remote buyers participate and you get a revenue share.

### 🏅 Digital Event Badges
Exclusive digital collectible badges for attendees who check in. Creates FOMO for future events and builds loyalty.

### 📈 Advanced Analytics
Detailed attendee demographics: what they collect, how engaged they are, where they're coming from. Use data to improve future events and attract sponsors.

### 🎯 Co-Marketing
We promote your event to our user base through in-app placements, push notifications, email newsletters, and social media.
