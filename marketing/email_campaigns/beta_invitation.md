## Sequence Strategy Notes

### Tone Progression:
- **Email 1:** Celebratory, exclusive, informative
- **Email 2:** Warm, social proof, gentle urgency
- **Email 3:** Direct, honest, final — no manipulation, just clarity

### Key Principles:
- Never beg or guilt-trip — maintain dignity and respect
- Make the exclusivity real (limited cohorts, actual waitlist)
- Personalization is critical — use real data about why they were chosen
- The founder's personal voice builds trust
- Always offer an easy out with no negative consequences

### Segmentation:
- **High-value waitlist** (influencers, large collections): Extend deadline generously, add personal video message
- **Standard waitlist:** Follow sequence as written
- **Referred by existing member:** Mention the referrer by name in Email 1

### Post-Activation:
- Immediately transition to Welcome Sequence (welcome_sequence.md)
- Add to private beta Slack/Discord channel
- Send "Getting Started with Beta" guide

### Post-Expiration (if not claimed):
- Move back to waitlist
- Send a "no hard feelings" email with option to rejoin waitlist
- May re-invite in future cohorts
