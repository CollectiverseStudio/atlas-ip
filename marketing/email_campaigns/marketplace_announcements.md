## 2. First Listing Guide

**Trigger:** 24 hours after seller verification, no listings yet  
**Subject Line:** Your first listing: a step-by-step walkthrough 📋  
**Preview Text:** List your first item in under 3 minutes. We'll walk you through every step.

---

**Body:**

Hi {{first_name}},

You're verified and ready to sell — let's get your first item listed! It takes less than 3 minutes, and we'll walk you through every step.

---

**Step-by-step: Creating your first listing**

### Step 1: Choose your item
Pick something you're ready to sell. Pro tip: Start with an item you know well — condition, history, approximate value.

### Step 2: Photos (the most important part!)
- **Minimum 3 photos** (front, back, detail)  
- **Natural lighting** works best  
- **Show any flaws** — honesty builds trust and reduces returns  
- **Include scale** — a coin next to a ruler, a card in a holder  
- **Pro tip:** Smart Capture can auto-generate your listing photos  

### Step 3: Title & Description
- **Title:** Be specific. "1989 Upper Deck Ken Griffey Jr. #1 PSA 9" beats "Baseball card"
- **Description:** Include everything a buyer needs to decide:
  - Condition (use standard grading where applicable)
  - Measurements or specifications
  - Provenance (where you got it, how long you've had it)
  - Any defects or repairs
  - What makes it special

### Step 4: Pricing
- **Check Price Intelligence** for recent comparable sales
- **Set a fair price** — or enable "Make an Offer" for flexibility
- **Consider auction format** for rare or hard-to-price items

### Step 5: Shipping
- **Specify shipping cost** (or offer free shipping — buyers love it)
- **Select carriers** you'll use
- **Note handling time** — how quickly you'll ship after purchase

### Step 6: Review & Publish
- Double-check everything
- Hit **Publish** — your item is now live!

## Template Usage & Personalization Notes

### Trigger Logic:
| Email | Trigger | Audience |
|-------|---------|----------|
| Seller Onboarding | Seller verification complete | New sellers |
| First Listing Guide | 24hr post-verification, no listings | Inactive new sellers |
| Item Sold | Payment confirmed | Active sellers |
| Buyer Welcome | First purchase complete | New buyers |
| New Items | New listings in followed categories | Active browsers |
| Weekly Digest | Sunday/Monday scheduled | All marketplace users |

### Personalization Requirements:
- Category preferences from user profile and browsing history
- Wish list matches for "New Items" emails
- Seller reputation and stats for transaction emails
- Recent market data for pricing context
- Behavioral data for frequency optimization

### Frequency Caps:
- "New Items" alerts: Maximum 3x per week (adjustable by user)
- Weekly Digest: 1x per week
- Transaction emails: Unlimited (essential communications)
- Seller onboarding: One-time sequence
