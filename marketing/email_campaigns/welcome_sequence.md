## Email 5: Create Your First Passport

**Send Time:** Day 7  
**Subject Line:** Your collection deserves a passport 🛂✨  
**Preview Text:** Create a stunning, shareable showcase of your best pieces.

---

**Body:**

Hi {{first_name}},

You've been on Collectiverse for a week now — and your collection is looking great! Time to show it off.

**Introducing Passports** — your collection's highlight reel.

A Passport is a curated, shareable showcase of your collection (or a subset of it). Think of it as your collector's portfolio — beautiful, organized, and designed to impress.

**Use Passports to:**

🖼️ **Showcase** — Create themed galleries (e.g., "My Vintage Star Wars Collection")  
🔗 **Share** — Send a link to friends, post on social media, or embed on your website  
📋 **Document** — Great for insurance records, estate planning, or selling  
🏆 **Flex** — Let the community see your best pieces  

**Creating a Passport is simple:**

1. Select items from your collection  
2. Choose a layout and theme  
3. Add descriptions and stories  
4. Publish and share!  

**Pro tip:** The best Passports tell a story. Add notes about how you found each piece, what it means to you, or its history.

## Segmentation Notes

- If user completes the action before receiving the email, skip or replace with congratulatory version
- If user hasn't added any items by Day 3, insert a "need help?" email before Atlas AI
- High-engagement users (5+ items added) should receive Email 7 earlier (Day 10)
- Users who import large collections (50+ items) should get a "power user" variant of the sequence
