### 11.4 Death Verification Process
Upon initiation of an estate claim, the following process applies:

1. **Claim Initiation:** A designated trusted contact or beneficiary submits a claim through the Platform.
2. **Required Documentation:** The claimant must provide:
   - Certified copy of the death certificate
   - Government-issued ID of the claimant
   - Documentation establishing the claimant's identity as a designated beneficiary or trusted contact (matching Platform records)
   - If applicable, letters testamentary or court documentation
3. **Verification Period:** Collectiverse verifies submitted documentation (typically 14-30 business days).
4. **Notification:** If the deceased has other trusted contacts, they are notified of the claim.
5. **Transfer Execution:** Upon successful verification, collection access transfers to designated beneficiaries per the deceased's instructions.
6. **Passport Trust Update:** Provenance records are updated to reflect the inheritance transfer.
