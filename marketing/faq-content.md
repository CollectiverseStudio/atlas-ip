## Getting Started

### 1. What is Collectiverse?

Collectiverse is the world's first universal collectibles platform — a single place to catalog, authenticate, buy, sell, and protect your collections across 14 categories: trading cards, comics, coins, stamps, watches, music, sneakers, toys, art, books, autographs, video games, tickets, and memorabilia. Think of it as an all-in-one collector's toolkit with AI-powered identification, blockchain-backed provenance, a marketplace, live streaming, event discovery, and estate planning.

### 2. How do I create an account?

Visit collectiverse.studio and click "Get Started." You can sign up with your email address or use Google/Apple sign-in. The Free tier gives you immediate access to basic collection management, the store directory, event discovery, and community features. No credit card is required for the Free tier.

### 3. Is Collectiverse free to use?

Yes! The Free tier lets you catalog items, browse the marketplace, use the store finder, discover events, and participate in the community at no cost. Premium features like AI appraisals, professional photo editing, and blockchain certificate minting require credits, available through our Pro ($9.99/month, 30 credits) or Business ($29.99/month, 100 credits) subscriptions.

### 4. What platforms is Collectiverse available on?

Collectiverse is available as:
- **Web app:** collectiverse.studio (works on any browser)
- **Progressive Web App (PWA):** Install directly from your browser for a native-like experience
- **iOS:** Available on the App Store
- **Android:** Available on Google Play

We also have dedicated apps for Events (events.collectiverse.studio) and Live streaming (live.collectiverse.studio).

### 5. What collecting categories does Collectiverse support?

We support 14 categories: Trading Cards, Comics, Coins, Stamps, Watches, Music (vinyl, CDs, cassettes), Sneakers, Toys & Action Figures, Art, Books (first editions, rare books), Autographs, Video Games, Tickets (concerts, sports events), and Memorabilia (sports, entertainment, historical).

## Collections

### 6. How do I add items to my collection?

Three ways:
1. **Smart Capture:** Take a photo of your item — Atlas AI identifies it automatically and fills all fields (name, year, set, manufacturer, condition indicators, variant details).
2. **Catalog Search:** Search our 5.75M+ item reference catalog and add items with one tap.
3. **Manual Entry:** Create custom entries for items not yet in our catalog.

### 7. What is Smart Capture?

Smart Capture uses Atlas AI's computer vision to identify collectibles from a single photograph. Point your camera at a trading card, comic book, coin, or any supported item — Atlas will identify it, cross-reference our 5.75 million+ item catalog, auto-populate all relevant fields, and provide an estimated market value. It works across all 14 categories.

### 8. Can I organize my collection into sub-collections or sets?

Absolutely. You can create unlimited sub-collections, organize by category, set, year, grade, value, or any custom criteria. You can also build registry sets for competition on our leaderboards and track set completion percentages.

### 9. Can I import my collection from other platforms?

Yes. We support CSV/spreadsheet imports from most popular collection management tools. Our import wizard maps your existing data fields to Collectiverse fields, and Atlas AI can help fill any gaps. Check our import guide for platform-specific instructions.

### 10. How is my collection's value calculated?

We aggregate real-time sales data from major marketplaces, auction houses, and our own platform to calculate fair market values. Atlas AI provides buy/hold/sell recommendations based on market trends, historical sales, population reports, and seasonal demand patterns. Values update regularly and you can view value trends over time for individual items and your overall portfolio.

## Passport Trust

### 11. What is Passport Trust?

Passport Trust is our provenance and authentication system — like CarFax for collectibles. It creates a permanent, verifiable history for each item including: ownership chain, authentication records, grading history, condition changes, and sale history. This builds buyer confidence and protects against fraud.

### 12. How does Passport Trust verify authenticity?

Passport Trust combines multiple verification layers:
- **AI Counterfeit Detection:** Atlas AI compares items against known authentic examples to flag inconsistencies
- **Professional Authentication Records:** Links to PSA, BGS, CGC, and other grading service certifications
- **Blockchain Certificates:** Immutable NFT certificates minted on Solana via Metaplex
- **Community Verification:** Trusted collector consensus on item authenticity
- **Ownership History:** Complete chain of custody from first registration

### 13. Is Passport Trust mandatory for all items?

No. You can catalog items without Passport Trust records. However, items with complete Passport Trust provenance receive a trust badge, appear higher in marketplace search results, and typically sell for more because buyers have confidence in their authenticity and history.

### 14. Can I transfer Passport Trust records when I sell an item?

Yes — that's the entire point. When you sell an item through Collectiverse, the Passport Trust record transfers to the buyer automatically, adding the new ownership record to the chain. The buyer receives the full provenance history, and you're added as a verified previous owner.

## AI Features

### 19. Who is Atlas?

Atlas is our AI assistant — a friendly chibi robot character (blue and white armor, gold star, green crescent eyes, blue hexagonal chest emblem with a white "C") who serves as your collecting companion. Atlas powers Smart Capture identification, counterfeit detection, market valuations, buy/hold/sell recommendations, and answers your collecting questions. Think of Atlas as a knowledgeable friend who happens to be an expert in all 14 collecting categories.

### 20. How accurate is Atlas AI for identification and valuation?

Atlas is trained on millions of collectible images and references our 5.75 million+ item catalog. Identification accuracy is highest for items well-represented in our catalog (major trading card sets, popular comics, common coins). For rare or obscure items, Atlas provides its best match with a confidence score and may suggest manual verification. Valuations are based on real recent sales data and should be treated as estimates — actual sale prices depend on condition, market timing, and buyer demand.

### 21. Can Atlas detect counterfeits?

Yes. Atlas analyzes visual characteristics including printing patterns, color accuracy, paper/card stock texture, holographic elements, and known counterfeit markers. When Atlas detects potential authenticity concerns, it flags the item with specific reasons (e.g., "Font spacing inconsistent with authentic 1st Edition stamps" or "Holographic pattern does not match known PSA-certified examples"). This is a supplementary tool — for high-value items, we always recommend professional third-party authentication.

### 22. What does "Buy/Hold/Sell" intelligence mean?

Atlas analyzes market trends, recent sales velocity, seasonal patterns, population reports, and upcoming catalysts (anniversaries, movie releases, player performance) to recommend whether now is a good time to buy, hold, or sell a specific item. These are data-driven suggestions, not financial advice — market conditions can change rapidly.

## Marketplace

### 23. How does buying and selling work on Collectiverse?

Sellers list items with photos, descriptions, and pricing (fixed price, best offer, or auction). Buyers browse, search, filter by category/condition/price, and purchase directly. Payment is processed securely through Stripe. Passport Trust records transfer automatically at sale. Our marketplace supports single items, lots, and bulk listings for Business tier users.

### 24. What are the fees for selling?

Please refer to our current fee schedule on the marketplace settings page. Fees are competitive with major platforms and vary by subscription tier — Business subscribers enjoy reduced selling fees as part of their plan benefits.

### 25. How does dispute resolution work?

If a buyer and seller disagree on an item's condition or authenticity, either party can open a dispute. Our resolution process includes:
1. Communication period (buyer and seller attempt to resolve directly)
2. Evidence review (photos, Passport Trust records, tracking info)
3. Collectiverse mediation (our team reviews evidence and makes a determination)

Passport Trust records serve as powerful evidence in disputes, which is another reason to maintain provenance records.

## Estate Planning

### 29. What is Estate Planning on Collectiverse?

Our Estate Planning feature ensures your collections are protected and properly transferred in the event of your passing. You can:
- Designate beneficiaries for your entire collection or specific items/sub-collections
- Provide instructions for how items should be handled (keep, sell, donate)
- Include special notes about sentimental items and their stories
- Set up trusted contacts who can initiate the transfer process

This is a unique feature designed for collectors whose items have significant financial and sentimental value.

### 30. How does the beneficiary transfer process work?

When a beneficiary or trusted contact initiates an estate claim:
1. A death verification process is triggered (requiring official documentation)
2. Collectiverse reviews and verifies the claim
3. Collection access is transferred to designated beneficiaries
4. Passport Trust records update to reflect the inheritance transfer
5. Beneficiaries receive full collection management access

The process is designed to be respectful, secure, and legally compliant. Beneficiaries do not need existing Collectiverse accounts — they'll be guided through account creation during the transfer.

## Technical

### 31. How does the blockchain/NFT system work?

When you mint a certificate of authenticity, we create an NFT on the Solana blockchain using the Metaplex protocol. This creates a permanent, publicly verifiable record that cannot be altered or deleted. The NFT contains item metadata, authentication records, and links to provenance data. You don't need a crypto wallet to use Collectiverse — we handle the blockchain infrastructure. However, if you have a Solana wallet, you can view and manage your certificates there as well.

### 32. Is my data secure?

We employ industry-standard security practices including:
- **Encryption:** All data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
- **Authentication:** Multi-factor authentication available on all accounts
- **Infrastructure:** Hosted on AWS with enterprise-grade security
- **Payments:** PCI-DSS compliant processing through Stripe (we never store card numbers)
- **Privacy:** GDPR, CCPA, and COPPA compliant (see our Privacy Policy)

### 33. What happens to my data if I cancel my subscription?

If you cancel your subscription, you retain access to your Free tier features. Your collection data, Passport Trust records, and blockchain certificates remain intact — they belong to you. You simply lose access to premium features (credits, advanced AI, bulk tools). You can re-subscribe at any time to restore premium access. If you delete your account entirely, we remove your personal data per our retention policy, but blockchain records remain on-chain (they are immutable by nature).

### 34. Is there an API for developers?

We offer API access for Business tier subscribers who want to integrate Collectiverse data with their own systems (inventory management, POS systems, websites). API documentation is available at docs.collectiverse.studio. Rate limits and available endpoints vary by plan.

### 35. What's the minimum age to use Collectiverse?

Collectiverse requires users to be 13 years or older, in compliance with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act). Users under 18 may need parental consent for certain features including marketplace transactions and blockchain minting. See our Terms of Service for complete age-related policies.
