## PRINT SPECIFICATIONS

- **Size:** 3.5" x 2" (US standard) or 85mm x 55mm (EU)
- **Bleed:** 0.125" on all sides
- **Safe zone:** 0.125" inset from trim
- **Paper stock:** 16pt matte with soft-touch lamination
- **Finish options:**
  - Option A: Soft-touch matte + spot UV on logo and Atlas
  - Option B: Soft-touch matte + gold foil stamp on logo
  - Option C: Standard matte (budget)
- **Corners:** Rounded (2mm radius) preferred
