# Atlas Mascot Marketing Campaign

## Character Reference

**Atlas** — The official mascot and AI guide of Collectiverse
- Cute chibi robot with large head, small body
- White helmet/head with blue accents
- Gold star on top of helmet
- Green crescent-shaped eyes (friendly expression)
- Orange mouth/speaker grille
- Blue hexagonal chest emblem with white "C" logo
- Personality: Friendly, optimistic, helpful, knowledgeable, enthusiastic about collecting
