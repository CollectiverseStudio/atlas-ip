# 🛡️ Passport — The Digital Identity for Every Collectible

**CarFax for Collectibles. Because "trust me, it's real" isn't good enough.**

### What Is a Passport?

Every item in Collectiverse can have a Passport — a permanent, verifiable record that travels with the item forever. It's the complete biography of a collectible.

### What a Passport Contains:

| Record Type | What's Tracked |
|-------------|---------------|
| **Ownership Chain** | Every owner, date acquired, transfer method |
| **Authentication Records** | PSA, BGS, CGC certs, AI verification, community consensus |
| **Grading History** | Every grade received, from every service, with cert numbers |
| **Condition Timeline** | Changes in condition over time |
| **Sale History** | Every transaction, every price, every platform |
| **Provenance Notes** | Special history, celebrity ownership, notable events |

### How Passports Build Trust

When you sell an item through Collectiverse, the complete Passport transfers to the buyer automatically. They see everything — every authentication, every owner, every grade. That transparency commands premium prices.

### Trust Tiers

Items earn trust badges based on their Passport completeness:

**Unverified → Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum**

Higher trust = higher marketplace visibility = faster sales at better prices.

### The Verification Ladder

PassportTrust combines multiple layers of verification to create unbreakable confidence:

| Layer | What It Does |
|-------|-------------|
| **AI Counterfeit Detection** | Atlas compares items against known authentic examples — printing patterns, holographics, paper stock, color accuracy |
| **Professional Certifications** | Links to PSA, BGS, CGC, and other grading service records with cert number verification |
| **Blockchain Certificates** | Immutable NFT records on Solana |

Every Passport receives a Trust Score (0–100), broken down by factors:

- **Provenance** — how complete is the ownership history?
- **Certification** — professional grading linked?
- **Possession** — verified in hand recently?
- **History** — how long has this been tracked?
- **Community** — peer verification?

### Improve Your Score

Atlas provides personalized recommendations: *"Add a photo → +8 points" • "Link PSA cert → +15 points" • "Verify possession → +12 points"*

Think of it as a **credit score for collectibles** — familiar, gamified, and motivating.
