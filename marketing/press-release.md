# PRESS RELEASE

## Collectiverse Launches World's First AI-Powered Universal Collectibles Platform With "CarFax for Collectibles" Provenance System

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**Wilmington, DE — [DATE]** — Collectiverse (collectiverse.studio), the comprehensive digital platform for collectors, today announced the public launch of its universal collectibles management and marketplace platform spanning 14 collecting categories. Featuring a groundbreaking Passport Trust provenance system, AI-powered identification and valuation, and the industry's largest multi-category reference catalog at over 5.75 million items, Collectiverse represents the most ambitious technology platform ever built for the $400+ billion global collectibles market.

---

### The "CarFax for Collectibles"

At the heart of Collectiverse is the **Passport Trust** system — a comprehensive provenance and authentication framework that creates an immutable ownership history for every collectible. Like CarFax revolutionized used car purchasing by providing vehicle history reports, Passport Trust gives collectors confidence in authenticity, ownership chain, and condition history for items across trading cards, comics, coins, stamps, watches, music, sneakers, toys, art, books, autographs, video games, tickets, and memorabilia.

"The collectibles industry has a $3 billion annual counterfeiting problem, and collectors deserve better than a handshake and a hope," said **Marcus Chen, Founder and CEO of Collectiverse**. "Passport Trust creates an unbreakable chain of provenance — from the moment an item enters our ecosystem, every authentication, every grade, every ownership transfer is permanently recorded. We're bringing the trust infrastructure that exists in real estate and automotive to the world of collectibles."

---

### Atlas: AI That Speaks Collector

Collectiverse introduces **Atlas**, an AI assistant purpose-built for collectors. Powered by computer vision trained on millions of collectible items, Atlas can identify items from a single photograph, estimate current market values, detect potential counterfeits, and provide buy/hold/sell recommendations based on real-time market intelligence.

The platform's **Smart Capture** technology allows collectors to photograph any item and have Atlas automatically identify it, populate all relevant fields (year, manufacturer, edition, condition indicators, variant details), and cross-reference it against the 5.75 million+ item reference catalog — a process that previously required manual research across multiple databases.

### A Complete Ecosystem

Collectiverse delivers a unified experience across three purpose-built applications:

- **Collectiverse** (collectiverse.studio) — Collection management, Passport Trust, marketplace, AI tools, store directory, and community features
- **Collectiverse Events** (events.collectiverse.studio) — Event discovery, QR check-in, show schedules, and dealer maps with PWA and native app support
- **Collectiverse Live** (live.collectiverse.studio) — Live streaming, live selling, live breaks, and live auctions with integrated payment processing

Additional platform capabilities include:
- **Registry Competition** — PSA-style leaderboards where collectors compete for top registry sets
- **Estate Planning** — Digital inheritance tools ensuring collections pass to designated beneficiaries
- **Price Intelligence** — Market-driven buy/hold/sell recommendations across all 14 categories
- **Blockchain Certificates** — NFT certificates of authenticity minted on Solana via Metaplex
