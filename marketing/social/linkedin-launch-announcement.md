After [X months] of building, testing, and obsessing over every detail — we've launched the platform we always wished existed as collectors ourselves.

**What is Collectiverse?**

It's the system of record for collectibles — a single platform that lets collectors catalog, authenticate, value, buy, sell, and protect their collections across 14 categories: trading cards, comics, coins, stamps, watches, music, sneakers, toys, art, books, autographs, video games, and memorabilia.

**Why we built it:**

The collectibles market has grown into a $400B+ industry, but the infrastructure hasn't kept up. Collections live in spreadsheets. Provenance is a handshake. Counterfeits are rampant. And when something happens to a collector, their family is left with boxes of "stuff" and no guidance.

We believe every collectible deserves a verified identity, and every collector deserves professional-grade tools — regardless of whether they have 10 items or 10,000.

**What makes Collectiverse different:**

🛡️ **Passport Trust System** — Think CarFax for collectibles. Every item gets a verified provenance record: authentication chain, ownership history, condition tracking, and optional blockchain certification on Solana. When an item changes hands, its Passport travels with it.

🤖 **Atlas AI** — Our purpose-built AI that identifies collectibles from photos, estimates current market values, and detects counterfeits. Trained on 5.75M+ items across all 14 categories. One photo → full catalog entry with valuation.

📊 **Price Intelligence** — Real market data with buy/hold/sell recommendations. Not what someone listed it for — what it actually sells for, right now.

🎥 **Live Selling & Breaks** — Native live streaming with built-in commerce. No more cobbling together OBS + PayPal + spreadsheets.

🏪 **Store Directory** — 18,622 collectible stores searchable by category and location. Free listings for store owners.

📅 **Events Discovery** — Find card shows, conventions, and meetups with QR check-in and live streaming.

🏛️ **Estate Planning** — Digital inheritance tools so your collection and its value are preserved for the next generation.

**The model:**

- **Free tier** — Full platform access. This isn't a trial.
- **Pro ($9.99/mo)** — Unlimited AI, advanced analytics, blockchain Passports.
- **Business ($29.99/mo)** — Store management, live selling tools, team access.

**What's next:**

We're building fast with our community. Marketplace with Passport-verified transactions, mobile apps, API access, and advanced portfolio analytics are all on the roadmap.

**The mission:**

We're building infrastructure for an industry that's been underserved by technology. Collectors deserve the same level of tooling that investors have for stocks, that car buyers have with CarFax, and that homeowners have with property records.
