## Slide 10: Passport & Trust

**Title:** CarFax for Collectibles

**Key Message:** Blockchain-backed provenance tracking creates an immutable ownership chain — building trust and increasing resale value.

**Supporting Points:**
- Every item gets a digital passport: verifiable ownership history from owner to owner
- Blockchain-anchored (Solana) — tamper-proof and permanent
- Authentication integration: link PSA, BGS, CGC, and other grading records
- Condition history: track changes over time with photo evidence
- Transfer on sale: passport follows the item to the new owner automatically
- Insurance-ready documentation: instant proof of ownership and value
- Increases buyer confidence and resale premiums (15-25% estimated value uplift)

## Appendix Notes

### Presentation Design Guidelines
- **Color Palette:** Deep navy (#0A1628), Electric blue (#3B82F6), Gold accent (#F59E0B), Clean white (#FFFFFF)
- **Typography:** Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans (modern, clean, tech-forward)
- **Imagery:** High-quality collectible photography, clean UI mockups, data visualizations
- **Tone:** Confident but approachable. Technical credibility meets collector passion.
- **Atlas Mascot:** Use sparingly — title slide, AI slide, closing slide. Friendly, knowledgeable, trustworthy.

### Slide Timing (20-minute presentation)
- Slides 1–3: Problem & Market (3 min)
- Slides 4–6: Solution overview (3 min)
- Slides 7–11: Deep dive features (5 min)
- Slide 12–13: Business model (3 min)
- Slides 14–16: Tech & traction (3 min)
- Slides 17–19: Strategy & financials (2 min)
- Slide 20: The Ask (1 min)

### Customization Notes
- Replace `$X` values with final pricing decisions and financial projections
- Add specific team member bios if presenting to investors
- Adjust "The Ask" based on audience (investor vs. partner vs. beta recruitment)
- Consider separate "deep dive" appendix slides for technical audiences
