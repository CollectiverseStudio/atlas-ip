## The Problem

Collectors today use 5–10 disconnected tools to manage their hobby or business:

- **Spreadsheets** for inventory tracking
- **eBay/Whatnot** for selling (separate platforms, no integration)
- **Photo albums** for visual records
- **Grading portals** (PSA, BGS, CGC) for authentication
- **Paper notebooks** for valuations and wish lists
- **Nothing** for estate planning or provenance

There is no system of record for collectors. When a collection changes hands — through sale, inheritance, or loss — critical information is scattered or lost entirely. Families of deceased collectors regularly lose hundreds of thousands in value because they don't know what items are worth or how to sell them.
