## Understanding Passports & Trust Scores

### What is a Collectiverse Passport?

Your Passport is your collector identity — a verified profile that builds trust in the community. Think of it as your collecting resume.

### Passport Components

| Component | What It Shows | How to Earn |
|-----------|---------------|-------------|
| **Identity Verification** | You are who you say you are | Verify email + phone |
| **Transaction History** | Your buying/selling track record | Complete purchases |
| **Community Rating** | How others rate dealing with you | Get reviews from sellers |
| **Collection Verified** | You own what you claim | Scan and verify items |
| **Event Attendance** | Active community member | Check in at events |
| **Account Age** | Tenure in the community | Time |

### Trust Scores Explained

Your trust score (0-100) reflects your reliability as a buyer:

```
🟢 90-100  — Trusted Collector (top tier, fastest responses from sellers)
🟢 75-89   — Established (most seller features unlocked)
🟡 50-74   — Active (building your reputation)
🟡 25-49   — New (some purchase limits may apply)
⚪ 0-24    — Unverified (complete Passport steps to improve)
```

### Why Trust Scores Matter for Buyers

- **Higher scores = Better access**: Some exclusive listings require minimum trust scores
- **Seller confidence**: Sellers are more likely to accept your offers
- **Event priority**: Get early access to live sales and drops
- **Lower holds**: Payment holds reduce as trust increases
- **Community perks**: Exclusive groups, early feature access

### Building Your Trust Score

1. ✅ Complete Passport verification (email, phone, ID)
2. ✅ Complete your first purchase (and leave a review)
3. ✅ Add items to your collection
4. ✅ Attend or check in at an event
5. ✅ Be active — browse, save, follow sellers
6. ✅ Pay promptly — don't leave sellers waiting
7. ✅ Communicate clearly — respond to messages

## Making Offers

### How Offers Work

Many listings on Collectiverse have a "Make an Offer" option. Here's how to use it effectively:

#### Sending an Offer

1. Find an item with the **"Make Offer"** button
2. Enter your offer amount
3. Optionally add a message (e.g., "I'll pay immediately if accepted!")
4. Submit — the seller has 48 hours to respond

#### What Happens Next

| Seller Response | What You Do |
|-----------------|-------------|
| **Accepted** | You'll be prompted to pay — complete within 24 hours |
| **Countered** | Review their counter-offer, accept/decline/counter again |
| **Declined** | No obligation — find another item or try a higher offer |
| **Expired** | Seller didn't respond in 48 hours — offer canceled automatically |

### Offer Etiquette & Tips

✅ **DO:**
- Research comparable prices before offering
- Offer at least 70-80% of asking (unless it's clearly overpriced)
- Include a friendly message — sellers prefer dealing with nice people
- Bundle requests: "I'll offer $X for this card plus [other listing]"
- Pay immediately when accepted — sellers notice and remember

❌ **DON'T:**
- Lowball aggressively (50% or less of asking is generally disrespectful)
- Ghost after an accepted offer (this tanks your trust score)
- Send the same lowball to every seller
- Pressure sellers with "I can get it cheaper elsewhere"

### Bundle Offers

See multiple items from one seller? Create a **bundle offer**:
1. Add items to your cart from the same seller
2. Click "Offer on Bundle"
3. Enter your total offer for all items
4. Sellers love bundles — you'll often get a better deal

## Buying During Live Events

### What Are Collectiverse Events?

Live events happen both **in-person** (card shows, cons, meetups) and **online** (live breaks, flash sales, auctions). They're where the collector community comes alive.

### In-Person Events

#### Before the Event
- Check the **Events** tab for shows near you
- See which vendors/sellers will be there
- Build your want list and set a budget
- RSVP to unlock event-exclusive features

#### At the Event
1. **Check in** via QR code at the venue (boosts your trust score!)
2. **Scan the floor** — vendors show their booth inventory in-app
3. **Make offers** in person with digital payment (fast, secure)
4. **Log purchases** to your collection instantly via scanning
5. **Connect** — follow sellers and other collectors you meet

#### Event-Exclusive Features
- **Digital payment** — Pay sellers via app (no cash needed)
- **Real-time pricing** — Check comps on the spot before buying
- **Event chat** — Connect with other attendees in real-time
- **Purchase logging** — Add to your collection as you buy

### Online Live Events

- **Live Breaks**: Watch cases/boxes opened live, claim your spot
- **Flash Sales**: Time-limited deals from top sellers
- **Auctions**: Bid in real-time on rare items
- **Show & Tell**: Community showcases (not buying, but great for discovery)

### Tips for Event Buying

1. **Set a budget before you go** — events are exciting, overspending is easy
2. **Know your prices** — Use the app to check comps before committing
3. **Inspect carefully** — In-person is your chance to see condition firsthand
4. **Ask questions** — Good sellers welcome collector conversation
5. **Get there early** — Best selection is always early in the show

## Protection Guarantees

### The Collectiverse Buyer Promise

We want you to buy with confidence. Here's how we protect you:

#### 🛡️ Item Authenticity Guarantee

- **Verified sellers** undergo Passport screening
- **AI-powered listing analysis** flags suspicious items
- **Community reporting** — collectors police their own
- **Dispute resolution** — If it's fake, you get your money back. Period.

#### 🛡️ Condition Guarantee

- **"As Described" Policy** — Item must match the listing description and photos
- **Graded items** — If the grade is wrong, full refund
- **Undisclosed damage** — If the seller hid flaws, you're protected
- **Photo documentation** — Always photograph items upon receipt

#### 🛡️ Delivery Guarantee

- **Tracking required** for all shipped items over $20
- **Lost in transit** — Covered (seller must provide tracking)
- **Damaged in shipping** — Covered (document with photos immediately)
- **Never arrives** — Full refund after delivery window + 5 days

#### 🛡️ Payment Protection

- **Escrow-style holds** — Sellers don't receive payment until you confirm receipt (for orders over $50)
- **Chargeback assistance** — We work with you, not against you
- **No ghost sellers** — Verified payment accounts required for all sellers

### How to File a Claim

If something goes wrong:

1. **Contact the seller first** — Most issues are resolved directly (and fast)
2. **Open a case** — If no response in 48 hours, open a case from your order
3. **Provide evidence** — Photos, tracking info, screenshots
4. **Resolution** — Our team reviews within 24-48 hours
5. **Refund** — If the claim is valid, money back to your original payment method

### Resolution Timeline

| Issue | Response Time | Typical Resolution |
|-------|---------------|-------------------|
| Item not as described | 24-48 hours | Return for full refund |
| Item never arrived | After delivery window + 5 days | Full refund |
| Fake/counterfeit | 24 hours (priority) | Immediate refund, seller flagged |
| Damaged in shipping | 24-48 hours | Refund or partial refund |
