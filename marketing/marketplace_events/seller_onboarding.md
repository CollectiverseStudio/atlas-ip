## Setting Up Your Store

### Step 1: Create Your Seller Account

1. Sign up at **collectiverse.com/sell** (or upgrade your existing buyer account)
2. Complete your **Collectiverse Passport** — this builds trust with buyers
3. Choose your **store name** (tip: make it memorable and searchable)
4. Upload a **store logo** and **banner image** (1200×300px recommended)

### Step 2: Configure Your Store Profile

```
Store Settings Checklist:
☐ Store name and description
☐ Logo and banner uploaded
☐ Business hours (for live chat/support)
☐ Shipping origins and policies
☐ Return policy
☐ Payment methods enabled
☐ Categories you sell in
☐ Social links (Instagram, YouTube, etc.)
```

### Step 3: Set Your Policies

**Shipping Policy**
- Define your handling time (1-3 business days recommended)
- Set shipping methods and rates (or offer free shipping thresholds)
- Specify international shipping availability

**Return Policy**
- Choose: No returns, 7-day, 14-day, or 30-day returns
- Define condition requirements for returns
- Specify who pays return shipping

**Store Description**
Write 2-3 paragraphs about:
- What you specialize in
- Your experience/credentials as a collector/dealer
- What buyers can expect (response time, packaging quality, etc.)

## Creating Listings

### Single Item Listings

#### Required Fields

| Field | Tips |
|-------|------|
| **Title** | Be specific: "2023 Topps Chrome Refractor #150 Shohei Ohtani PSA 10" |
| **Category** | Choose the most specific category available |
| **Condition** | Raw, Graded (specify company + grade), Sealed, etc. |
| **Price** | Set your asking price (or enable offers) |
| **Photos** | Minimum 2, recommended 4-8 (front, back, corners, edges) |
| **Description** | Include anything not visible in photos |

#### Optional (But Recommended) Fields

- **Passport Tags** — Link to your Passport collection data for provenance
- **Comparable Sales** — Reference recent comps to justify pricing
- **Shipping Weight** — Enables accurate calculated shipping
- **Accept Offers** — Set minimum offer threshold (e.g., 80% of asking)
- **Quantity** — For non-unique items (supplies, common cards, etc.)

#### Pro Tips for Listings

1. **Title Formula**: [Year] [Brand] [Set] [Parallel/Insert] [Card #] [Player/Character] [Condition/Grade]
2. **Front-load keywords** — Buyers search, they don't browse
3. **Mention flaws honestly** — Trust = repeat customers
4. **Cross-reference** — Link related items ("See my other Ohtani listings")

### Bulk Listing Tools

#### CSV Upload

For sellers with large inventories:

1. Download our **CSV template** from Store Settings → Bulk Tools
2. Fill in your inventory (supports up to 10,000 items per upload)
3. Upload and review — our system auto-categorizes and flags issues
4. Confirm and publish

**CSV Template Columns:**
```
title, category, subcategory, condition, grade_company, grade, 
price, quantity, description, photo_url_1, photo_url_2, 
photo_url_3, photo_url_4, weight_oz, shipping_profile, 
accept_offers, min_offer_percent
```

#### Scan & List (Mobile)

1. Open the Collectiverse app
2. Tap **"Scan to List"**
3. Point your camera at the item (cards, comics, figures)
4. Our AI identifies the item and pre-fills listing details
5. Review, adjust price, add photos, and publish

#### eBay Import

Already selling on eBay? Import your active listings:

1. Go to Store Settings → Integrations → eBay
2. Authorize your eBay account
3. Select listings to import (all or filtered)
4. Review mapping and pricing
5. Publish — items are now listed on both platforms with inventory sync

## Pricing Strategies

### Market-Based Pricing

- **Check recent sales** — Use Collectiverse's built-in comp tool
- **Factor in condition** — A PSA 10 vs. PSA 9 can be 3-10x difference
- **Consider timing** — Prices spike during player hot streaks, movie releases, etc.
- **Watch trends** — Our analytics show what's gaining/losing value

### Pricing Models

| Strategy | Best For | How It Works |
|----------|----------|--------------|
| **Fixed Price** | Confident in value | Set it and forget it |
| **Accept Offers** | Want to negotiate | Set minimum, accept/counter/decline |
| **Auction** | Rare/uncertain value | Let the market decide |
| **Bundle Pricing** | Moving volume | Discount for multi-item purchases |
| **Event Pricing** | Live shows/sales | Time-limited deals during events |

### Pricing Tips

1. **Price competitively but fairly** — Undercutting destroys the market for everyone
2. **Bundle slow movers** — Package commons with a desirable hit
3. **Use "Make an Offer"** — You'd be surprised what people will pay
4. **Seasonal awareness** — Football cards peak Aug-Feb, baseball peaks Mar-Oct
5. **Graded premium** — Raw cards at 60-70% of graded value is a good baseline

## Photography Tips

### Equipment (You Don't Need Much)

- **Smartphone** (2020 or newer) — perfectly fine for most items
- **Good lighting** — Natural daylight OR a $30 ring light
- **Clean background** — White poster board, black velvet, or a photo box ($20-40)
- **Card stand or holder** — Keeps items upright and centered

### Shot List for Cards/Comics

| Shot | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Front straight-on** | Primary listing image |
| **Back straight-on** | Verification/condition |
| **Corners close-up** | Condition documentation |
| **Edge/surface close-up** | Surface condition, centering |
| **In-holder/slab** | If graded, show the full slab |
| **Lifestyle shot** | Optional: in a display, in context |

### Photography Do's & Don'ts

✅ **DO:**
- Shoot in natural or diffused light
- Fill the frame (item should be 80%+ of image)
- Show any flaws clearly (builds trust!)
- Use consistent backgrounds across your store
- Clean items and holders before photographing

❌ **DON'T:**
- Use flash (causes glare, especially on slabs/toploaders)
- Photograph on busy/cluttered surfaces
- Over-edit or filter images
- Use stock photos for actual items
- Hide damage with angles or cropping

### Image Specifications

- **Minimum**: 800×800px
- **Recommended**: 1600×1600px
- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **File size**: Under 5MB per image
- **Orientation**: Square crop preferred (displays best on mobile)

## Shipping Guide

### Packaging Best Practices

#### Cards (Raw)
1. Card in penny sleeve
2. Penny sleeve into top loader
3. Top loader in team bag (sealed)
4. Place in bubble mailer with cardboard stiffener
5. "Do Not Bend" + "Non-Machinable" on exterior

#### Cards (Graded/Slabbed)
1. Slab in fitted sleeve or bubble wrap
2. Place in small box (not a bubble mailer for slabs!)
3. Fill void with packing paper or bubble wrap
4. "Fragile" on exterior

#### Comics
1. Comic in bag with board
2. Place between two pieces of cardboard (larger than comic)
3. Tape cardboard sandwich together
4. Place in appropriately sized mailer/box

#### Figures/Larger Items
1. Wrap in bubble wrap
2. Place in box with minimum 2" of padding on all sides
3. Fill ALL voids — items shouldn't shift when shaken
4. Double-box for items over $100

### Shipping Cost Strategies

| Strategy | Pros | Cons |
|----------|------|------|
| **Free Shipping** | More sales, simpler | You absorb cost |
| **Flat Rate** | Predictable for buyers | May over/under charge |
| **Calculated** | Accurate, fair | Can scare off buyers |
| **Free over $X** | Encourages larger orders | Still absorb cost sometimes |

### Recommended Carriers

- **USPS First Class** — Best for items under 13oz ($3-5)
- **USPS Priority** — Best for packages, includes $50 insurance
- **UPS Ground** — Best for heavy/multiple items
- **FedEx** — Best for high-value, time-sensitive shipments
- **Pirate Ship** — Discounted USPS/UPS rates (integrates with Collectiverse)

### Shipping Timeline

- **Handling time**: Ship within your stated handling time (1-3 days recommended)
- **Upload tracking**: Add tracking number as soon as available
- **Communicate delays**: Life happens — message the buyer if you'll be late

## Payment Processing

### Accepted Payment Methods

Collectiverse supports **6 payment options** so you never lose a sale:

| Method | Setup | Payout Speed | Fees |
|--------|-------|--------------|------|
| **Stripe** | Connect account in Settings | 2 business days | 2.9% + $0.30 |
| **PayPal** | Link your PayPal email | Instant to PayPal | 2.9% + $0.30 |
| **Venmo** | Link via PayPal Business | Instant to Venmo | 1.9% + $0.10 |
| **Apple Pay** | Enabled via Stripe | 2 business days | 2.9% + $0.30 |
| **Google Pay** | Enabled via Stripe | 2 business days | 2.9% + $0.30 |
| **Square** | Connect Square account | 1-2 business days | 2.6% + $0.10 |

### Setting Up Payments

#### Stripe (Recommended as Primary)
1. Go to Store Settings → Payments → Stripe
2. Click "Connect with Stripe"
3. Complete Stripe onboarding (takes ~5 minutes)
4. Once connected, Apple Pay and Google Pay are automatically enabled

#### PayPal + Venmo
1. Go to Store Settings → Payments → PayPal
2. Click "Connect with PayPal"
3. Authorize your PayPal Business account
4. Enable Venmo as a checkout option (toggle in PayPal settings)

#### Square
1. Go to Store Settings → Payments → Square
2. Click "Connect with Square"
3. Authorize your Square account
4. Ideal if you already use Square at card shows/in-store

### Payment Tips

- **Enable all methods** — Different buyers prefer different payment methods
- **Stripe + PayPal** covers 95%+ of online transactions
- **Square** is great if you also sell in person at events
- **Apple Pay / Google Pay** dramatically improve mobile checkout conversion

## Multi-Channel Selling: eBay Integration

### How It Works

```
┌─────────────────┐     ┌──────────────────┐     ┌─────────────┐
│  Collectiverse  │◄───►│  Sync Engine     │◄───►│    eBay     │
│  Marketplace    │     │  (Real-time)     │     │  Marketplace│
└─────────────────┘     └──────────────────┘     └─────────────┘
```

**List Once. Sell Everywhere.**

- Create a listing on Collectiverse → it appears on eBay
- Sell on eBay → inventory updates on Collectiverse
- Sell on Collectiverse → listing ends on eBay
- Update price on either → syncs to the other

### Setting Up eBay Sync

1. **Connect**: Store Settings → Integrations → eBay → Authorize
2. **Map Categories**: Match Collectiverse categories to eBay categories
3. **Set Rules**: Choose default eBay listing format (auction vs. BIN)
4. **Import or Sync**: Pull existing eBay listings in, or push Collectiverse out
5. **Monitor**: Dashboard shows sync status, errors, and cross-platform sales

### Sync Features

- ✅ Inventory quantity sync (real-time)
- ✅ Price sync (optional — some sellers price differently per platform)
- ✅ Order notifications across platforms
- ✅ Sold item tracking in unified dashboard
- ✅ Listing end/relist automation
- ⚙️ Coming soon: Whatnot, COMC, MySlabs integration

**Day 1-2**: Publish initial listings, share your store link on social media
**Day 3-4**: Respond to any questions quickly (response time affects ranking)
**Day 5-7**: Add more inventory, adjust pricing based on views/watchers

### Ongoing Best Practices

- **List regularly** — Fresh inventory boosts store visibility
- **Respond fast** — Under 2 hours is the gold standard
- **Ship promptly** — Same-day or next-day when possible
- **Ask for reviews** — After successful deliveries, a polite ask goes far
- **Stay active** — Participate in community events and live sales
