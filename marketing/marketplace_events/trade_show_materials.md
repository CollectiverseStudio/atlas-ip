## 🎬 Demo Script (2 Minutes)

### Setup
- Phone or tablet mounted/visible to audience
- Collectiverse app open
- Have a few cards/items ready to scan

### Script

---

**[0:00 - 0:15] HOOK**

"Hey! Quick question — how do you keep track of your collection right now? Spreadsheet? Shoeboxes? Your brain? *[pause for laughs/nods]*

What if I told you there's an app that does it all — scanning, tracking, buying, selling, and finding shows like this one — in one place? Let me show you."

**[0:15 - 0:45] SCAN & CATALOG**

"Watch this. I'm going to scan this card right here..."

*[Scan a card with the app]*

"Boom — instantly identified. It pulls up the card details, recent sales, current market value. I tap 'Add to Collection' and it's cataloged. My whole collection is tracked with real-time values. Took two seconds."

**[0:45 - 1:15] BUY & SELL**

"Now say I want to sell this one. I tap 'List for Sale,' it pre-fills everything from the scan, I set my price, add a photo, and publish. It's now on the Collectiverse marketplace AND can sync to my eBay store if I want."

"For buying — every seller on here is Passport-verified. You know who you're dealing with. Six payment options, buyer protection, the whole deal."

**[1:15 - 1:45] EVENTS & COMMUNITY**

"Here's what's cool for today — if you check in to this event *[show check-in]*, you can see every vendor here, what they've got listed, and find exactly what you're looking for without walking the entire floor blind."

"Plus you're building your trust score just by checking in and being active. Higher trust means better access to exclusive drops and events."

**[1:45 - 2:00] CLOSE**

"It's totally free to sign up. Scan this QR code *[point to sign]* and you're in. First 100 listings are free if you want to sell. And we're doing a giveaway today — just sign up and you're entered."

"Any questions? We're here all day. Happy collecting!"

---

### Demo Tips

- **Energy**: Be genuinely enthusiastic — collectors respond to passion
- **Speed**: The scan demo is the "wow" moment — make sure it's fast
- **Customize**: Swap the demo card for whatever's relevant to the event (Pokémon at a TCG show, etc.)
- **Invite interaction**: "Want to try scanning one of YOUR cards?"
- **Business card handoff**: Give them something physical to take

## 📋 Event Logistics Checklist

### What to Bring

```
Equipment:
☐ Retractable banner (main backdrop)
☐ Table signs (4-6 tent cards)
☐ Flyers (100-200 per day)
☐ Business cards (200+ per day)
☐ QR code standee (large, visible)
☐ Tablet/phone for demos (charged + backup battery)
☐ WiFi hotspot (don't rely on venue WiFi)
☐ Extension cord + power strip
☐ Giveaway items (in locked container until drawing)

Supplies:
☐ Branded tablecloth
☐ Stickers/swag for casual giveaway
☐ Clipboard for email signups (backup if QR fails)
☐ Candy/mints (draws people to the table)
☐ Pens (branded if possible)
☐ Tape, scissors, zip ties (setup fixes)

Demo Items:
☐ Cards/items for live scanning demo
☐ Sample listings to show marketplace
☐ Pre-loaded search results to show discovery
```

### Booth Setup Priority

1. **Banner visible from aisle** (first impression)
2. **QR code at eye level** (easy to scan while walking)
3. **Demo device ready and charged** (be ready for walkups)
4. **Flyers accessible** (front of table, take-one style)
5. **Giveaway signage prominent** (crowd driver)
