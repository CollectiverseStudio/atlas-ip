**Section 6: FAQ**

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Why is my store already in the directory?**
A: We built a comprehensive directory of collectible stores from public business listings. Your basic info (name, address, category) is listed to help collectors find stores near them. Claiming gives you full control.

**Q: Does claiming cost anything?**
A: No. Claiming your profile is free, forever. Online selling has marketplace fees (only when you sell), but there's no monthly cost.

**Q: What if the info in my listing is wrong?**
A: That's exactly why you should claim it! Once claimed, you control all the details. Update anything that's incorrect.

**Q: Do I have to sell online?**
A: Nope! Many stores claim simply to manage their listing, get reviews, and post events. Online selling is entirely optional.

**Q: Can I unclaim later?**
A: Yes, though we're not sure why you'd want to. 😄 You can revert to an unclaimed listing at any time with no penalty.

**Q: How do I verify ownership?**
A: We verify via the phone number, email, or physical address associated with the business. Usually takes under 5 minutes.

## Outreach Strategy Notes

### Segmentation

| Segment | Priority | Messaging Focus |
|---------|----------|-----------------|
| Stores with eBay presence | High | eBay sync, multi-channel |
| Stores hosting events | High | Event promotion tools |
| Stores with social following | Medium | Community, visibility |
| Stores with no online presence | Medium | Easy first step to online |
| Stores in major metro areas | High | Local collector density |
| Stores in collector-heavy categories | High | Targeted audience |

### Outreach Channels

1. **Email** (primary) — Templates above
2. **Direct mail** (postcard) — For stores without email
3. **Phone** (for high-priority) — Personal touch for top targets
4. **In-person** (at shows) — When attending events with store owners present
5. **Social DM** — For stores active on Instagram/Facebook

### Success Metrics

- **Open rate target**: 35%+ (personalization drives this)
- **Claim rate target**: 10% of opened emails
- **Time to claim**: Track days from first email to claim
- **Activation rate**: % of claimed stores that list inventory within 30 days
- **Revenue attribution**: Sales from claimed store listings
