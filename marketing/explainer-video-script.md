# Explainer Video Script: "What is Collectiverse?"

**Format:** Animated explainer video  
**Duration:** 90 seconds  
**Style:** 2D motion graphics with 3D Atlas character  
**Tone:** Energetic, trustworthy, community-focused  
**Music:** Upbeat electronic with subtle retro-gaming undertones  

---

## SCENE 1: The Problem (0:00–0:15)

**[VISUAL]:** Montage of collectibles — trading cards scattered on a table, comics in long boxes, coins in flips, sneakers on shelves. Camera pulls back to show a collector surrounded by chaos — spreadsheets, sticky notes, receipts, multiple apps open on phone and laptop.

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Atlas character** appears in every scene as a guide/companion
- **Color palette:** Primary blue (#1e40af), white, metallic silver, gold accents
- **Typography:** Modern sans-serif (suggest Inter or Montserrat), bold for callouts
- **Transitions:** Smooth morph/dissolve transitions between scenes
- **Sound design:** Subtle UI sounds when Atlas scans/identifies items, satisfying "ding" on Passport Trust stamp
- **Accessibility:** Closed captions included; narration pacing allows for subtitle reading
- **Aspect ratios needed:** 16:9 (YouTube/web), 9:16 (TikTok/Reels/Shorts), 1:1 (social feeds)
- **Estimated production time:** 3-4 weeks with professional animation studio
