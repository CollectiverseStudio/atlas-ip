# Collectiverse — TikTok/Reels Video Concepts (20)

**Platforms:** TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts
**Duration:** 15–60 seconds (aim for under 45 where possible)
**Brand Voice:** Energetic, authentic, satisfying, collector-native
**Key Principles:**
- Hook in first 2 seconds (pattern interrupt)
- Show, don't tell (visual-first medium)
- Satisfying moments (scans, reveals, organization)
- Authentic — not overproduced
- Atlas personality woven in where natural

## PRODUCTION NOTES

### General TikTok/Reels Best Practices

**Hook principles:**
- First 1-2 seconds determine if people keep watching
- Pattern interrupt (unexpected statement, surprising visual)
- Create a "wait, what?" or "I need to see this" moment
- Text on screen + voice = double engagement

**Filming tips:**
- Phone-quality is fine (authentic > polished)
- Good lighting is non-negotiable
- POV/first-person perspective performs well
- Show real hands, real items, real reactions
- Quick cuts keep attention (no shot longer than 5 seconds)

**Audio strategy:**
- Trending sounds boost discoverability
- Original audio for series content (becomes recognizable)
- Voiceover for explanation videos
- Music + text overlay for montages

**Posting strategy:**
- Post 1-2x daily during growth phase
- Best times: 7-9 AM, 12-2 PM, 7-9 PM (test and iterate)
- Respond to every comment in first hour
- Use 3-5 hashtags (mix trending + niche + branded)
- Cross-post to Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts
