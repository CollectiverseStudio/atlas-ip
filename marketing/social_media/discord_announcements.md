## Announcement 2 — Smart Capture Launch

@everyone

# 📸 SMART CAPTURE IS LIVE

Point your camera. Get results. That simple.

**What Atlas can do now:**
• Identify cards, coins, comics, and more from a single photo
• Auto-fill every catalog field
• Estimate condition and grade range
• Provide current market value
• Flag potential counterfeits

**How to try it:**
1. Open Collectiverse
2. Tap "Smart Capture" (camera icon)
3. Point at any collectible
4. Watch Atlas work ✨

**Pro tips for best results:**
- Good lighting (natural light is ideal)
- Flat surface, minimal background clutter
- Include the full item (edges to edges)
- For graded items, include the label

Drop your first Smart Capture results in #show-and-tell — we want to see what Atlas identifies for you!

## Announcement 6 — Digital Passport Feature

# 🛡️ DIGITAL PASSPORTS ARE HERE

Think of this as a **CarFax for your collectibles.**

Every item can now have a permanent, verifiable record:
- 📋 Authentication history
- 👤 Ownership chain
- 📸 Condition documentation over time
- ⛓️ Blockchain verification (optional, on Solana)
- 📄 Insurance-ready documentation

**How it works:**
1. Go to any item in your collection
2. Tap "Create Passport"
3. Add authentication details (grading info, purchase records)
4. Done — your Passport is live and verifiable

**When you sell:** The Passport transfers with the item. Buyers can verify provenance instantly.

**When you insure:** Export Passport data for documentation.

**When you pass it down:** The entire history travels with the item forever.

This is the feature we're most proud of. Trust infrastructure for collectibles. Finally.

Questions? Drop them in #passport-help!

## DISCORD BEST PRACTICES

### Frequency
- Major announcements: 2-4x per month
- Minor updates: weekly in #changelog
- Community events: bi-weekly
- Never more than 2 @everyone pings per week

### Formatting
- Use headers (#) for scan-ability
- Emojis for visual breaks
- Bullet points for features
- Bold for key phrases
- Keep @everyone for truly important moments

### Engagement
- Always end with a question or call-to-action
- Monitor responses for 2+ hours after posting
- Heart-react comments to acknowledge them
- Pin important follow-up info
- Create threads for extended discussions
