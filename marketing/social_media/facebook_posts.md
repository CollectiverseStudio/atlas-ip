## COLLECTOR TIPS (Posts 11–18)

### Post 11 — Storage Tips (Short)

💡 **Quick tip:** Direct sunlight is your collection's worst enemy. UV damage is irreversible. Invest in UV-filtering cases or keep items away from windows.

Your future self (and your collection's value) will thank you.

#CollectorTips #Collectiverse #PreserveYourCollection

---

### Post 12 — Grading Prep

📝 **Thinking about getting something graded?**

Before you submit:
1. Check comps — is the grading fee worth the value bump?
2. Inspect centering carefully (it's the #1 reason for grade misses)
3. Use a jeweler's loupe to check for surface issues
4. Take photos BEFORE sending for your records
5. Document everything in your Collectiverse Passport

Pro tip: Atlas AI can give you a condition estimate before you submit. Save yourself a $25 surprise.

#GradingTips #PSA #BGS #CGC #Collectiverse

### Post 13 — Insurance Reminder (Short)

⚠️ Your homeowner's insurance probably doesn't cover your collection. Seriously.

Most policies cap "collectibles" at $1,500–$2,500 total. If your collection is worth more, you need a scheduled rider or dedicated policy.

Step one: know what you have and what it's worth. That's what we're here for.

#CollectorTips #InsureYourCollection #Collectiverse
